Arjuna asks to see the cosmic form11.1–11.4

The delusion is gone; now Arjuna longs to see the imperishable form itself.

11.1My delusion is gone
By the supreme secret word concerning the Self which you have spoken out of compassion, this delusion of mine is gone.
11.2I have heard of your origin and passing
I have heard in full from you of the origin and dissolution of beings, and of your imperishable majesty. As you have described yourself, O supreme Lord, so I desire to see your divine form.
11.3I want to see your form
Just as you have described yourself, O highest Lord, I want to see your universal form.
11.4If you think I can bear it, show me
If you think it possible for me to behold it, O Lord, master of yoga, then show me your imperishable Self.

Kṛṣṇa grants the divine eye11.5–11.8

Behold my hundreds and thousands of forms — and, since mortal eyes cannot see them, receive a divine eye.

11.5Behold my hundreds and thousands of forms
Behold my forms, O Pārtha, by hundreds and by thousands, of many kinds, divine, of various colours and shapes.
11.6The Vasus, Rudras, Aśvins
See the sun-gods, the Vasus, the Rudras, the twin Aśvins, the storm-winds — wonders you have never seen before.
11.7See the whole world in my body
Behold now the whole universe, moving and unmoving, standing together here in my body — and whatever else you desire to see.
11.8I give you a divine eye
But you cannot see me with these eyes of yours; I give you a divine eye — behold my sovereign yoga.

Sañjaya describes a thousand suns11.9–11.14

The narrator's voice: if a thousand suns should rise together, that might resemble the splendour of that great Self.

11.9The great Lord of Yoga showed his form
Having spoken thus, the great Lord of yoga, Hari, showed to Arjuna his supreme sovereign form — with many mouths and eyes, many wondrous sights, many divine ornaments and uplifted divine weapons.
11.10Marvels beyond count
— with innumerable faces and eyes, marvels beyond count, celestial ornaments, and weapons raised on every side.
11.11If a thousand suns should rise
Wearing divine garlands and robes, anointed with divine perfumes, all-wonderful, resplendent, boundless, facing every direction. If the light of a thousand suns were to blaze forth at once in the sky, that might resemble the splendour of that great Self.
11.12Brighter than a thousand suns at once
If a thousand suns blazed up together in the sky, it would not match the glory of that great being.
11.13The whole universe in one place
There Arjuna beheld the whole universe, divided in many ways, standing as one in the body of the God of gods.
11.14Hair on end, bowing with folded hands
Then Arjuna, filled with amazement, his hair standing on end, bowed his head to the God and spoke with folded hands.

I See All the Gods11.15–11.18

In your body I see all beings — Brahmā on his lotus seat, the seers, the serpents — a blazing mass of radiance without beginning or end.

11.15I see all beings in your body
I see all the gods in your body, O God, and the hosts of all kinds of beings; Brahmā the Lord seated on the lotus, all the seers and the divine serpents.
11.16Infinite arms, no beginning, middle or end
I see you with countless arms, bellies, mouths and eyes, of boundless form on every side; I see no end, no middle, and no beginning in you, O Lord of all, universal form.
11.17A blazing mass of radiance
I see you crowned, armed with mace and discus, a mass of radiance blazing everywhere — hard to look upon, immeasurable, like blazing fire and sun on every side.
11.18You are the imperishable, the supreme to be known
You are the imperishable, the supreme to be known; you are the ultimate resting place of this universe; you are the undying guardian of eternal dharma — you are the primal Person, I hold.

11.53 ↗
नाहं वेदैर्न तपसा न दानेन न चेज्यया । शक्य एवंविधो द्रष्टुं दृष्टवानसि मां यथा
nāhaṃ vedairna tapasā na dānena na cejyayā । śakya evaṃvidho draṣṭuṃ dṛṣṭavānasi māṃ yathā
I am not to be seen in this way through the Vedas, nor through austerity, nor through gifts, nor through worship; only in the way you have seen me can I be seen.“This vision is not won by Vedic study, austerity, charity, or ritual — I am seen thus only as you have seen me.”
न वेदद्वारा, न तपस्याद्वारा, न दानद्वारा, न पूजाद्वारा म यस रूपमा देखिन सक्छु; तिमीले मलाई जसरी देख्यौ, त्यसरी नै मात्र म देखिन्छु।
न वेदों से, न तपस्या से, न दान से, न पूजा से मैं इस रूप में देखा जा सकता हूँ; केवल उसी प्रकार, जिस प्रकार तूने मुझे देखा, मैं देखा जाता हूँ।
11.54 ↗
भक्त्या त्वनन्यया शक्य अहमेवंविधोऽर्जुन । ज्ञातुं द्रष्टुं च तत्त्वेन प्रवेष्टुं च परन्तप
bhaktyā tvananyayā śakya ahamevaṃvidho’rjuna । jñātuṃ draṣṭuṃ ca tattvena praveṣṭuṃ ca parantapa
But through single-pointed devotion I can be known, seen and entered in truth, O Arjuna, in this very form.“Yet by undivided devotion I may be truly known, seen, and entered, Arjuna — in this very form.”
तर हे अर्जुन, एकनिष्ठ भक्तिद्वारा म यसै रूपमा साँच्चै जानिन, देखिन र प्रवेश गर्न सकिन्छु।
किन्तु हे अर्जुन, एकनिष्ठ भक्ति से मैं इसी रूप में सचमुच जाना, देखा और प्रवेश किया जा सकता हूँ।
11.55 ↗
श्रीभगवानुवाच । मत्कर्मकृन्मत्परमो मद्भक्तः सङ्गवर्जितः । निर्वैरः सर्वभूतेषु यः स मामेति पाण्डव
śrībhagavānuvāca । matkarmakṛnmatparamo madbhaktaḥ saṅgavarjitaḥ । nirvairaḥ sarvabhūteṣu yaḥ sa māmeti pāṇḍava
He who does my work, who holds me as the supreme goal, who is devoted to me, free from attachment, without enmity toward any being — he comes to me, O Pāṇḍava.“One who works for me, making me his supreme goal, devoted to me, free of clinging and of enmity toward all — he reaches me, Arjuna.”
जसले मेरो कर्म गर्छ, मलाई परम लक्ष्य मान्छ, मप्रति भक्त छ, आसक्तिरहित छ, र कुनै प्राणीप्रति द्वेष राख्दैन — हे पाण्डव, ऊ मकहाँ आउँछ।
जो मेरा कर्म करता है, मुझे परम लक्ष्य मानता है, मुझ में भक्त है, आसक्ति से रहित है, और किसी प्राणी से द्वेष नहीं रखता — हे पाण्डव, वह मुझे प्राप्त होता है।
