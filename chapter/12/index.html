Arjuna asks: manifest or unmanifest?12.1–12.2

Who knows yoga better — those who worship the manifest with devotion, or those who worship the imperishable Unmanifest?

12.1Which knows Yoga better?
Those devotees who, ever united, worship you — and those who worship the imperishable unmanifest — which of these are the better knowers of yoga?
12.2Those ever-united in me are best in yoga
Those who, fixing their minds on me, ever united, worship me with supreme faith — them I deem the most yoked.

arjuna uvāca । evaṃ satatayuktā ye bhaktāstvāṃ paryupāsate । ye cāpyakṣaramavyaktaṃ teṣāṃ ke yogavittamāḥ
Arjuna said: Those devotees who, ever united, worship you — and those who worship the imperishable unmanifest — which of these are the better knowers of yoga?Arjuna asks: “Which are the greater yogis — those who worship you with devotion, or those who worship the formless, imperishable Absolute?”
अर्जुनले भने: सधैं युक्त भएर तपाईंको पूजा गर्ने भक्तहरू — र अविनाशी अव्यक्तको पूजा गर्नेहरू — यीमध्ये कुन योगका राम्रो ज्ञाता हुन्?

The unmanifest path is harder12.3–12.5

Those who worship the imperishable, unthinkable and immovable also reach me — but greater is their toil.

12.3Some worship the imperishable unmanifest
But those who worship the imperishable, the indefinable, the unmanifest — the all-pervading, unthinkable, unchanging, immovable, eternal —
12.4They too reach me
— controlling all the senses, of equal mind everywhere, rejoicing in the welfare of all beings — they too reach me.
12.5Greater is the toil of those set on the unmanifest
Greater is the toil of those whose minds are attached to the unmanifest; the unmanifest is hard to attain by embodied beings.

जिनके मन अव्यक्त में आसक्त हैं, उनका परिश्रम अधिक है; देहधारियों के लिए अव्यक्त की प्राप्ति कठिन है।

I swiftly deliver my devotees12.6–12.7

For those who surrender all actions to me and meditate on me, I become the deliverer from the ocean of death and rebirth.

12.6Those who surrender all to me
But those who surrender all actions to me, intent on me, worshipping me with undivided yoga and meditating on me,
12.7I become their deliverer from the ocean of death
— for them, whose minds are fixed on me, O Pārtha, I swiftly become the deliverer from the ocean of death and rebirth.

12.6 ↗

ye tu sarvāṇi karmāṇi mayi sannyasya matparāḥ । ananyenaiva yogena māṃ dhyāyanta upāsate

किन्तु जो सब कर्म मुझ में अर्पण करते हैं, मुझ में ही तत्पर होकर, अविभाजित योग से मेरी पूजा करते हैं और मेरा ध्यान करते हैं,

Give me your mind and your intellect12.8

The direct instruction, spoken to Arjuna alone: place the mind in me, settle the intellect in me — and you will dwell in me. Everything after this verse is a concession to those who cannot.

12.8Fix your mind on me alone
Fix your mind on me alone; establish your intellect in me; then you shall live in me alone — of this there is no doubt.

मय्येव मन आधत्स्व मयि बुद्धिं निवेशय । निवसिष्यसि मय्येव अत ऊर्ध्वं न संशयः
mayyeva mana ādhatsva mayi buddhiṃ niveśaya । nivasiṣyasi mayyeva ata ūrdhvaṃ na saṃśayaḥ

केवल ममै मन लगाऊ; ममै बुद्धि स्थिर गर; तब तिमी ममै बाँच्नेछौ — यसमा कुनै सन्देह छैन।
केवल मुझ में ही मन लगा; मुझ में ही बुद्धि स्थिर कर; तब तू मुझ में ही रहेगा — इसमें कोई सन्देह नहीं।

If you cannot fix your mind on me12.9–12.12

The ladder of concession: if not steady meditation, then practice; if not practice, then work for me; if not that, then renounce the fruit.

12.9Then seek me by repeated practice
If you cannot fix your mind steadily on me, then practise the yoga of repetition — seek to reach me by practice, O Dhanañjaya.
12.10If not, work for my sake
If you are unable to practise even that, be devoted to working for me; even performing actions for my sake, you shall attain perfection.
12.11If not even that, renounce the fruit
If you cannot do even this, then, taking refuge in my yoga, renounce the fruit of all actions — with the self controlled.
12.12Better than knowledge is renunciation of fruit
Better indeed is knowledge than practice; better than knowledge is meditation; better than meditation is renunciation of the fruit of action — for from renunciation comes peace, immediately.

The devotee who is dear to me12.13–12.16

The portrait of the devotee: hating no being, friendly and compassionate, undisturbed by the world and not disturbing it.

12.13Hating no being, friendly and compassionate
He who hates no being, is friendly and compassionate, free from possessiveness and ego, equal in pleasure and pain, forgiving —
12.14Ever content, resolved, given to me
— ever content, a yogi, self-controlled, of firm resolve, mind and intellect offered to me — that devotee of mine is dear to me.
12.15He by whom the world is not disturbed
He by whom the world is not disturbed, and who is not disturbed by the world — freed from joy, anger, fear and anxiety — he is dear to me.
12.16Unattached, pure, capable, untroubled
He who is unattached, pure, capable, indifferent, free from anxiety, renouncing all undertakings — that devotee of mine is dear to me.

Equal to friend and foe, to honour and dishonour, to cold and heat, pleasure and pain, free from attachment —
12.19Silent, satisfied with anything
— silent, content with anything, homeless, of steady mind, full of devotion — that man is dear to me.

12.17 ↗
यो न हृष्यति न द्वेष्टि न शोचति न काङ्क्षति । शुभाशुभपरित्यागी भक्तिमान्यः स मे प्रियः
yo na hṛṣyati na dveṣṭi na śocati na kāṅkṣati । śubhāśubhaparityāgī bhaktimānyaḥ sa me priyaḥ
He who neither rejoices nor hates, neither grieves nor desires, renouncing good and evil — that devoted one is dear to me.“One who neither delights nor despairs, neither mourns nor craves, letting go of good and evil — such a devotee is dear to me.”
जो न रम्छ न घृणा गर्छ, न शोक गर्छ न इच्छा राख्छ, असल र नराम्रो दुवै त्याग्ने — त्यो भक्त मलाई प्रिय छ।
जो न रमता है न घृणा करता है, न शोक करता है न इच्छा रखता है, शुभ और अशुभ दोनों को त्यागने वाला — वह भक्त मुझे प्रिय है।
12.18 ↗
समः शत्रौ च मित्रे च तथा मानापमानयोः । शीतोष्णसुखदुःखेषु समः सङ्गविवर्जितः
samaḥ śatrau ca mitre ca tathā mānāpamānayoḥ । śītoṣṇasukhaduḥkheṣu samaḥ saṅgavivarjitaḥ
Equal to friend and foe, to honour and dishonour, to cold and heat, pleasure and pain, free from attachment —“Equal toward friend and enemy, praise and blame, cold and heat, joy and sorrow, free from clinging —”
मित्र र शत्रु, मान र अपमान, जाडो र गर्मी, सुख र दुःखमा समान, आसक्तिरहित —
मित्र और शत्रु, मान और अपमान, सर्दी और गर्मी, सुख और दुःख में समान, आसक्ति-रहित —
12.19 ↗
तुल्यनिन्दास्तुतिर्मौनी सन्तुष्टो येन केनचित् । अनिकेतः स्थिरमतिर्भक्तिमान्मे प्रियो नरः
tulyanindāstutirmaunī santuṣṭo yena kenacit । aniketaḥ sthiramatirbhaktimānme priyo naraḥ
— silent, content with anything, homeless, of steady mind, full of devotion — that man is dear to me.“— silent, satisfied with whatever comes, unattached to place, steady-minded, devoted — such a person is dear to me.”
— मौन, जे पनि पाए त्यसैमा सन्तुष्ट, घरबारविहीन, स्थिर मन भएको, भक्तिले भरिएको — त्यो मानिस मलाई प्रिय छ।
— मौन, जो कुछ मिले उसी में संतुष्ट, गृह-रहित, स्थिर मन वाला, भक्ति से भरा — वह मनुष्य मुझे प्रिय है।

Follow this immortal dharma and be dearest to me12.20

The chapter's seal: those who follow this whole teaching with faith, holding me as the supreme goal, are not merely dear but EXCEEDINGLY dear to me.

12.20They who follow this immortal dharma are dearest
But those who follow this immortal dharma, as declared, with faith, making me the supreme goal — those devotees are exceedingly dear to me.
Read the verses
12.20 ↗
ये तु धर्म्यामृतमिदं यथोक्तं पर्युपासते । श्रद्दधाना मत्परमाः भक्तास्तेऽतीव मे प्रियाः
ye tu dharmyāmṛtamidaṃ yathoktaṃ paryupāsate । śraddadhānā matparamāḥ bhaktāste’tīva me priyāḥ
But those who follow this immortal dharma, as declared, with faith, making me the supreme goal — those devotees are exceedingly dear to me.“But those who follow this deathless dharma with faith, holding me as the highest goal — such devotees are most dear to me of all.”
तर जसले यो अमृत धर्मलाई, बताइएअनुसार, श्रद्धाले, मलाई परम लक्ष्य बनाएर अनुसरण गर्छन् — ती भक्तहरू मलाई अत्यन्त प्रिय छन्।
किन्तु जो इस अमृत धर्म का, कहे अनुसार, श्रद्धा से, मुझे परम लक्ष्य बनाकर अनुसरण करते हैं — वे भक्त मुझे अत्यन्त प्रिय हैं।
