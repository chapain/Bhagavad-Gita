The body is the field, the Self its knower13.1–13.2

Kṛṣṇa opens the teaching: the body is the field, know Me as the knower of the field in all fields — and the knowledge of field and knower is true knowledge.

13.1The body is the field
This body, O Kaunteya, is called the field; he who knows it, the sages call the knower of the field.
13.2Know me as the knower
Know me as the knower of the field in all fields; the knowledge of field and knower is my true knowledge.

Kṛṣṇa will describe the field13.3–13.4

Kṛṣṇa will describe the field — its nature, modifications and source; the seers have sung it in many ways.

13.3I shall declare
Hear from me in brief what the field is, what it is like, its modifications and source — and who he is and his powers.
13.4Sung by the seers
The seers have sung it in many ways, in diverse meters — and in the aphorisms of Brahman, well-reasoned and certain.

The field's twenty-four constituents13.5–13.6

The field comprises the great elements, ego, intellect, the unmanifest, the senses, and their objects — and desire, aversion, pleasure and pain.

13.5The elements of the field
The great elements, ego, intellect, the unmanifest, the ten senses and the one, and the five objects of the senses.
13.6Desire and aversion
Desire, aversion, pleasure, pain, the body, consciousness and constancy — this, in brief, is the field with its modifications.

The Means of Knowledge13.7–13.11

Humility, non-violence, service to the teacher, purity, steadfastness, dispassion, absence of ego, and constant meditation — these are declared knowledge.

13.7Humility, non-violence, service to the teacher
Humility, modesty, non-violence, forbearance, uprightness, service to the teacher, purity, steadfastness, self-control,
13.8Seeing the misery of birth
— disinterest in sense-pleasures, no ego, and the clear seeing of the misery of birth, death, age, and disease.
13.9Non-attachment and evenness
— non-attachment, non-clinging to son, wife and home, and constant even-mindedness toward gain and loss,
13.10Devotion, solitude, self-knowledge
— unwavering devotion to me, delight in solitude and distaste for crowds.
13.11The aim of truth-knowledge
— constant pursuit of knowledge of the Self, and seeing the aim of truth-knowledge: this is called knowledge; all else is ignorance.

The knowable is beginningless Brahman13.12

Kṛṣṇa declares the knowable: Brahman, beginningless, beyond being and non-being — the supreme reality.

13.12The knowable declared
I will declare the object of knowledge, knowing which one tastes immortality — the beginningless supreme Brahman, called neither being nor non-being.

Brahman is everywhere, with every face13.13–13.16

Brahman is all-hands and all-feet, all-eyes, all-heads, all-ears — pervading all, without senses yet sensing all, unattached yet upholding all.

13.13All hands, all feet
With hands and feet everywhere, eyes, heads and faces everywhere, ears everywhere — it stands, pervading all in the world.
13.14Yet has no senses
It appears to have all the senses' qualities, yet has no senses; it is unattached, yet supports all; beyond the guṇas, yet it enjoys the guṇas.
13.15Within and without, far and near
It is within and without all beings, unmoving and yet moving; so subtle it cannot be known — far away, and at the same time near.
13.16Undivided in the divided
Undivided, yet appearing divided among beings; know it as the sustainer of beings, and as the one that devours and brings forth.

जैसे सर्वव्यापी आकाश, सूक्ष्म होने पर भी, लिप्त नहीं होता, वैसे ही हर शरीर में स्थित आत्मा लिप्त नहीं होती।

The knower lights the whole field13.33–13.34

As the sun illumines the whole world, the knower of the field illumines the whole field; those who see this distinction reach the supreme.

13.33The sun and the field
As the one sun illumines this whole world, so the knower of the field illumines the whole field, O Bhārata.
13.34The final vision
Those who, with the eye of knowledge, see the distinction between field and knower, and the liberation from prakṛti — they reach the supreme.
Read the verses
13.33 ↗
यथा प्रकाशयत्येकः कृत्स्नं लोकमिमं रविः । क्षेत्रं क्षेत्री तथा कृत्स्नं प्रकाशयति भारत
yathā prakāśayatyekaḥ kṛtsnaṃ lokamimaṃ raviḥ । kṣetraṃ kṣetrī tathā kṛtsnaṃ prakāśayati bhārata
As the one sun illumines this whole world, so the knower of the field illumines the whole field, O Bhārata.“A single sun lights the whole world. In just that way, the one who knows the field lights up the field entire.”
जसरी एउटै सूर्यले यो सम्पूर्ण संसारलाई उज्यालो बनाउँछ, हे भारत, त्यसरी नै क्षेत्रज्ञले सम्पूर्ण क्षेत्रलाई उज्यालो बनाउँछ।
जैसे एक ही सूर्य इस सम्पूर्ण जगत् को प्रकाशित करता है, हे भारत, वैसे ही क्षेत्रज्ञ सम्पूर्ण क्षेत्र को प्रकाशित करता है।
13.34 ↗
क्षेत्रक्षेत्रज्ञयोरेवमन्तरं ज्ञानचक्षुषा । भूतप्रकृतिमोक्षं च ये विदुर्यान्ति ते परम्
kṣetrakṣetrajñayorevamantaraṃ jñānacakṣuṣā । bhūtaprakṛtimokṣaṃ ca ye viduryānti te param
Those who, with the eye of knowledge, see the distinction between the field and its knower, and the liberation from prakṛti — they reach the supreme.“Those who, with the eye of wisdom, discern the difference between the field and its knower, and the freedom from nature — they attain the highest.”
जसले ज्ञानको आँखाले क्षेत्र र क्षेत्रज्ञको भेद, र प्रकृतिबाट मुक्ति देख्छन् — तिनीहरू परम तत्त्वमा पुग्छन्।
जो ज्ञान के नेत्र से क्षेत्र और क्षेत्रज्ञ का भेद, और प्रकृति से मुक्ति देखते हैं — वे परम तत्त्व को प्राप्त होते हैं।
