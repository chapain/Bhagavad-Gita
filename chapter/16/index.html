The twenty-six divine qualities 16.1–16.3

Kṛṣṇa enumerates the twenty-six qualities of one born to the divine destiny — from fearlessness and purity to vigour, forgiveness and freedom from pride.

16.1Fearlessness and purity
Fearlessness, purity of heart, steadfastness in knowledge and yoga, charity, self-control, sacrifice, study of scripture, austerity and uprightness,
16.2Non-violence and truth
— non-violence, truth, freedom from anger, renunciation, peace, absence of slander, compassion, non-covetousness, gentleness, modesty and steadiness —
16.3Vigour and forgiveness
— vigour, forgiveness, fortitude, purity, freedom from malice and from pride — these belong to one born to the divine destiny, O Bhārata.
Read the verses
16.1 ↗
श्रीभगवानुवाच । अभयं सत्त्वसंशुद्धिर्ज्ञानयोगव्यवस्थितिः । दानं दमश्च यज्ञश्च स्वाध्यायस्तप आर्जवम्
śrībhagavānuvāca । abhayaṃ sattvasaṃśuddhirjñānayogavyavasthitiḥ । dānaṃ damaśca yajñaśca svādhyāyastapa ārjavam
The Blessed Lord said: Fearlessness, purity of heart, steadfastness in knowledge and yoga, charity, self-control, sacrifice, study of scripture, austerity and uprightness,Kṛṣṇa said: “Fearlessness, purity of heart, firmness in knowledge and meditation, charity, self-restraint, sacrifice, study, austerity, and honesty,”
श्रीभगवान्ले भन्नुभयो: अभय, हृदयको शुद्धता, ज्ञान र योगमा दृढता, दान, संयम, यज्ञ, शास्त्र-अध्ययन, तपस्या र सरलता,
श्री भगवान् ने कहा: अभय, हृदय की शुद्धि, ज्ञान और योग में स्थिरता, दान, दम, यज्ञ, शास्त्र-अध्ययन, तपस्या और सरलता,
16.2 ↗
अहिंसा सत्यमक्रोधस्त्यागः शान्तिरपैशुनम् । दया भूतेष्वलोलुप्त्वं मार्दवं ह्रीरचापलम्
ahiṃsā satyamakrodhastyāgaḥ śāntirapaiśunam । dayā bhūteṣvaloluptvaṃ mārdavaṃ hrīracāpalam
— non-violence, truth, freedom from anger, renunciation, peace, absence of slander, compassion, non-covetousness, gentleness, modesty and steadiness —“— harmlessness, truth, freedom from anger, letting go, calm, no backbiting, compassion, no greed, gentleness, humility, and firmness —”
— अहिंसा, सत्य, क्रोधरहितता, त्याग, शान्ति, निन्दारहितता, करुणा, अस्पृहता, कोमलता, लज्जा र स्थिरता —
— अहिंसा, सत्य, क्रोध-रहितता, त्याग, शान्ति, निन्दा-रहितता, करुणा, अस्पृहता, कोमलता, लज्जा और स्थिरता —
16.3 ↗
तेजः क्षमा धृतिः शौचमद्रोहो नातिमानिता । भवन्ति सम्पदं दैवीमभिजातस्य भारत
tejaḥ kṣamā dhṛtiḥ śaucamadroho nātimānitā । bhavanti sampadaṃ daivīmabhijātasya bhārata
— vigour, forgiveness, fortitude, purity, freedom from malice and from pride — these belong to one born to the divine destiny, O Bhārata.“— energy, patience, resolve, purity, and freedom from spite and vanity — these are the marks of one born to the divine nature.”
— तेज, क्षमा, धैर्य, शुद्धता, द्वेषरहितता र अभिमानरहितता — यी हे भारत, दैवी सम्पत्ति भएर जन्मेकाका चिह्न हुन्।
— तेज, क्षमा, धैर्य, शुद्धता, द्वेष-रहितता और अभिमान-रहितता — ये, हे भारत, दैवी सम्पदा से जन्मे की पहचान हैं।

The qualities of the demoniac 16.4

Over against the divine stands its opposite: hypocrisy, arrogance, pride, anger, harshness and ignorance — the marks of the demoniac birth.

16.4The six marks
Hypocrisy, arrogance, pride, anger, harshness and ignorance — these belong to one born to the demoniac destiny, O Pārtha.

16.4 ↗
दम्भो दर्पोऽतिमानश्च क्रोधः पारुष्यमेव च । अज्ञानं चाभिजातस्य पार्थ सम्पदमासुरीम्
dambho darpo’timānaśca krodhaḥ pāruṣyameva ca । ajñānaṃ cābhijātasya pārtha sampadamāsurīm
Hypocrisy, arrogance, pride, anger, harshness and ignorance — these belong to one born to the demoniac destiny, O Pārtha.“Hypocrisy, haughtiness, vanity, anger, rudeness, and ignorance — these mark the demonic nature.”
हे पार्थ, दम्भ, घमण्ड, अभिमान, क्रोध, कठोरता र अज्ञान — यी आसुरी सम्पत्ति भएर जन्मेकाका चिह्न हुन्।
हे पार्थ, दम्भ, दर्प, अभिमान, क्रोध, कठोरता और अज्ञान — ये आसुरी सम्पदा से जन्मे की पहचान हैं।

Divine frees, demoniac binds 16.5

The divine endowment leads to liberation, the demoniac to bondage; Kṛṣṇa reassures Arjuna that he is born to the divine.

16.5Liberation and bondage
The divine endowment leads to liberation, the demoniac to bondage — grieve not, O Pāṇḍava, for you are born to the divine destiny.

16.5 ↗
दैवी सम्पद्विमोक्षाय निबन्धायासुरी मता । मा शुचः सम्पदं दैवीमभिजातोऽसि पाण्डव
daivī sampadvimokṣāya nibandhāyāsurī matā । mā śucaḥ sampadaṃ daivīmabhijāto’si pāṇḍava
The divine endowment leads to liberation, the demoniac to bondage — grieve not, O Pāṇḍava, for you are born to the divine destiny.“The divine nature frees you; the demonic binds you. Do not grieve, Arjuna — you are born with the divine nature.”
दैवी सम्पत्तिले मुक्ति दिन्छ, आसुरीले बन्धन — हे पाण्डव, शोक नगर, किनकि तिमी दैवी सम्पत्ति लिएर जन्मेका छौ।
दैवी सम्पदा मुक्ति के लिए, आसुरी बन्धन के लिए है — हे पाण्डव, शोक मत कर, क्योंकि तू दैवी सम्पदा लेकर जन्मा है।

The demoniac deny God and truth 16.6–16.8

Two creations exist in this world. The demoniac know neither right action nor renunciation, and hold the world to be without truth, without foundation, without a Lord.

16.6The two creations
There are two creations of beings in this world — the divine and the demoniac; the divine has been described at length — now hear of the demoniac, O Pārtha.
16.7Without truth or purity
The demoniac do not know right action or renunciation; there is no purity, no conduct, no truth in them.
16.8The godless world-view
They say the world is unreal, without foundation, without a Lord — produced by mutual union, born of lust — what else?
Read the verses
16.6 ↗
द्वौ भूतसर्गौ लोकेऽस्मिन्दैव आसुर एव च । दैवो विस्तरशः प्रोक्त आसुरं पार्थ मे शृणु
dvau bhūtasargau loke’smindaiva āsura eva ca । daivo vistaraśaḥ prokta āsuraṃ pārtha me śṛṇu
There are two creations of beings in this world — the divine and the demoniac; the divine has been described at length — now hear of the demoniac, O Pārtha.“Two kinds of beings exist in this world — divine and demonic. I have described the divine; now hear of the demonic.”
यस संसारमा प्राणीहरूका दुई सृष्टिहरू छन् — दैवी र आसुरी; दैवी विस्तारमा भनियो — हे पार्थ, अब आसुरीको सुन।
इस संसार में प्राणियों की दो सृष्टियाँ हैं — दैवी और आसुरी; दैवी का विस्तार से वर्णन किया गया — हे पार्थ, अब आसुरी का सुन।
16.7 ↗
प्रवृत्तिं च निवृत्तिं च जना न विदुरासुराः । न शौचं नापि चाचारो न सत्यं तेषु विद्यते
pravṛttiṃ ca nivṛttiṃ ca janā na vidurāsurāḥ । na śaucaṃ nāpi cācāro na satyaṃ teṣu vidyate
The demoniac do not know right action or renunciation; there is no purity, no conduct, no truth in them.“The demonic do not know what should be done or renounced — they have no purity, no good conduct, no truth.”
आसुरीहरूले गर्नुपर्ने र नगर्नुपर्ने जान्दैनन्; तिनमा न शुद्धता छ, न सदाचार, न सत्य।
आसुरी लोग कर्तव्य और अकर्तव्य को नहीं जानते; उनमें न शुद्धि है, न सदाचार, न सत्य।
16.8 ↗
असत्यमप्रतिष्ठं ते जगदाहुरनीश्वरम् । अपरस्परसम्भूतं किमन्यत्कामहैतुकम्
asatyamapratiṣṭhaṃ te jagadāhuranīśvaram । aparasparasambhūtaṃ kimanyatkāmahaitukam
They say the world is unreal, without foundation, without a Lord — produced by mutual union, born of lust — what else?“They claim the world is false, without ground, without God — arising by chance from desire.”
तिनीहरू भन्छन्: संसार असत्य छ, आधाररहित छ, स्वामीरहित छ — परस्पर संयोगबाट उत्पन्न, कामनाबाट जन्मेको — अरू के होला र?
वे कहते हैं: संसार असत्य है, आधार-रहित है, ईश्वर-रहित है — परस्पर संयोग से उत्पन्न, कामना से जन्मा — और क्या?

Insatiable desire drives them to ruin 16.9–16.12

Holding that view, the ruined souls work for the world's destruction — driven by insatiable desire, boundless anxiety, and hundreds of fetters of hope.

16.9The ruined souls
Holding this view, these ruined souls of little understanding perform cruel deeds, enemies of the world, for its destruction.
16.10Insatiable desire, hypocrisy and pride
Given to insatiable desire, full of hypocrisy, pride and arrogance, holding false views through delusion, they act with impure resolves.
16.11Cares that end only in death
Given to boundless cares ending only in death, intent on the enjoyment of desires, thinking 'this is all' —
16.12The fetters of hope
— bound by hundreds of fetters of hope, given to desire and anger, they strive to amass wealth by unjust means for the gratification of lust.
Read the verses
16.9 ↗
एतां दृष्टिमवष्टभ्य नष्टात्मानोऽल्पबुद्धयः । प्रभवन्त्युग्रकर्माणः क्षयाय जगतोऽहिताः
etāṃ dṛṣṭimavaṣṭabhya naṣṭātmāno’lpabuddhayaḥ । prabhavantyugrakarmāṇaḥ kṣayāya jagato’hitāḥ
Holding this view, these ruined souls of little understanding perform cruel deeds, enemies of the world, for its destruction.“With this view, the lost, dull-witted souls do brutal things — enemies of the world, bent on ruining it.”
यो दृष्टि राखेर, यी अल्पबुद्धि नष्ट आत्माहरूले, संसारका शत्रु भएर, यसको विनाशका निम्ति क्रूर कर्म गर्छन्।
इस दृष्टि को धारण करके, ये अल्प-बुद्धि नष्ट आत्माएँ, जगत् के शत्रु होकर, उसके विनाश के लिए क्रूर कर्म करती हैं।
16.10 ↗
काममाश्रित्य दुष्पूरं दम्भमानमदान्विताः । मोहाद्गृहीत्वाऽसद्ग्राहान्प्रवर्तन्तेऽशुचिव्रताः
kāmamāśritya duṣpūraṃ dambhamānamadānvitāḥ । mohādgṛhītvā’sadgrāhānpravartante’śucivratāḥ
Given to insatiable desire, full of hypocrisy, pride and arrogance, holding false views through delusion, they act with impure resolves.“Slaves of never-satisfied craving, puffed with vanity and arrogance, they cling to wrong ideas and act with impure intentions.”
अतृप्त कामनामा डुबेका, दम्भ, घमण्ड र अहंकारले भरिएका, मोहले झूटा दृष्टि राख्ने तिनीहरू अशुद्ध संकल्पले कर्म गर्छन्।
अतृप्त कामना में डूबे, दम्भ, दर्प और अहंकार से भरे, मोह से झूठी दृष्टि रखने वाले, वे अशुद्ध संकल्पों से कर्म करते हैं।
16.11 ↗
चिन्तामपरिमेयां च प्रलयान्तामुपाश्रिताः । कामोपभोगपरमा एतावदिति निश्चिताः
cintāmaparimeyāṃ ca pralayāntāmupāśritāḥ । kāmopabhogaparamā etāvaditi niścitāḥ
Given to boundless cares ending only in death, intent on the enjoyment of desires, thinking 'this is all' —“Consumed by endless worries that end only at death, living for pleasure, they think, 'this is all there is' —”
मृत्युमा मात्र अन्त्य हुने असीम चिन्ताहरूमा डुबेका, कामनाको भोगमा तत्पर, 'यही नै सबै हो' भनी सोच्ने —
मृत्यु पर ही समाप्त होने वाली अपार चिन्ताओं में डूबे, कामनाओं के भोग में तत्पर, 'यही सब कुछ है' — ऐसा सोचने वाले —
16.12 ↗
आशापाशशतैर्बद्धाः कामक्रोधपरायणाः । ईहन्ते कामभोगार्थमन्यायेनार्थसञ्चयान्
āśāpāśaśatairbaddhāḥ kāmakrodhaparāyaṇāḥ । īhante kāmabhogārthamanyāyenārthasañcayān
— bound by hundreds of fetters of hope, given to desire and anger, they strive to amass wealth by unjust means for the gratification of lust.“— tied by a hundred ropes of hope, devoted to desire and anger, they pile up wealth by wrongful means for their pleasures.”
— सयौं आशाका बन्धनले बाँधिएका, कामना र क्रोधमा डुबेका, तिनीहरू भोगको निम्ति अन्यायपूर्ण उपायले धन जम्मा गर्न लाग्छन्।
— सैकड़ों आशाओं के बन्धनों से बँधे, कामना और क्रोध में डूबे, वे भोग की तृप्ति के लिए अन्यायपूर्ण साधनों से धन अर्जित करते हैं।

The boast of the deluded rich 16.13–16.15

In their own words: what I have gained, the enemies I have slain, the wealth and birth that make me lord — the self-praise of those deluded by ignorance.

16.13This I gained; more shall be mine
'This I gained today; this wish I shall fulfil; this wealth is mine — and more shall be mine,' they think —
16.14I am the Lord
'I have slain this enemy, and I will slay others too; I am the lord, I am the enjoyer, I am accomplished, powerful and happy,'
16.15Who else is like me
'I am rich and well-born; who else is like me? I will sacrifice, I will give, I will rejoice' — thus deluded by ignorance.
Read the verses
16.13 ↗
इदमद्य मया लब्धमिदं प्राप्स्ये मनोरथम् । इदमस्तीदमपि मे भविष्यति पुनर्धनम्
idamadya mayā labdhamidaṃ prāpsye manoratham । idamastīdamapi me bhaviṣyati punardhanam
'This I gained today; this wish I shall fulfil; this wealth is mine — and more shall be mine,' they think —‘I got this today; I will get that desire tomorrow; this is mine, and more will be mine,’ they boast —
'यो मैले आज पाएँ; यो इच्छा म पूरा गर्नेछु; यो धन मेरो छ — र अझ बढी मेरो हुनेछ,' तिनीहरू सोच्छन् —
'यह मैंने आज पाया; यह इच्छा मैं पूरी करूँगा; यह धन मेरा है — और और भी मेरा होगा,' वे सोचते हैं —
16.14 ↗
असौ मया हतः शत्रुर्हनिष्ये चापरानपि । ईश्वरोऽहमहं भोगी सिद्धोऽहं बलवान्सुखी
asau mayā hataḥ śatrurhaniṣye cāparānapi । īśvaro’hamahaṃ bhogī siddho’haṃ balavānsukhī
'I have slain this enemy, and I will slay others too; I am the lord, I am the enjoyer, I am accomplished, powerful and happy,'‘I have killed that foe, and I will kill the rest; I am the master, the enjoyer, successful, mighty, and happy,’
'मैले यो शत्रुलाई मारें, र अरूलाई पनि मार्नेछु; म स्वामी हुँ, म भोक्ता हुँ, म सिद्ध, शक्तिशाली र सुखी छु,'
'मैंने इस शत्रु को मारा, और दूसरों को भी मारूँगा; मैं स्वामी हूँ, मैं भोक्ता हूँ, मैं सिद्ध, बलवान् और सुखी हूँ,'
16.15 ↗
आढ्योऽभिजनवानस्मि कोऽन्योऽस्ति सदृशो मया । यक्ष्ये दास्यामि मोदिष्य इत्यज्ञानविमोहिताः
āḍhyo’bhijanavānasmi ko’nyo’sti sadṛśo mayā । yakṣye dāsyāmi modiṣya ityajñānavimohitāḥ
'I am rich and well-born; who else is like me? I will sacrifice, I will give, I will rejoice' — thus deluded by ignorance.“‘I am wealthy and noble; who can equal me? I will offer, give, and enjoy’ — so the ignorant delude themselves.”
'म धनी र उच्च कुलको छु; मजस्तो अरू को होला? म यज्ञ गर्नेछु, दान दिनेछु, रम्नेछु' — यसरी अज्ञानले मोहित हुन्छन्।
'मैं धनी और उच्च कुल का हूँ; मेरे समान और कौन है? मैं यज्ञ करूँगा, दान दूँगा, आनन्द मनाऊँगा' — इस प्रकार अज्ञान से मोहित होते हैं।

The Fall into Hell 16.16–16.17

Bewildered by many thoughts and caught in the net of delusion, they fall into a foul hell; their sacrifices are performed in name only.

16.16The net of delusion
Bewildered by many thoughts, wrapped in the net of delusion, devoted to sensual pleasures, they fall into a foul hell.
16.17Sacrifice in name only
Self-conceited, stubborn, filled with the pride of wealth, they perform sacrifices in name only, with hypocrisy, not according to the rule.
Read the verses
16.16 ↗
अनेकचित्तविभ्रान्ता मोहजालसमावृताः । प्रसक्ताः कामभोगेषु पतन्ति नरकेऽशुचौ
anekacittavibhrāntā mohajālasamāvṛtāḥ । prasaktāḥ kāmabhogeṣu patanti narake’śucau
Bewildered by many thoughts, wrapped in the net of delusion, devoted to sensual pleasures, they fall into a foul hell.“Torn by countless thoughts, caught in the web of delusion, addicted to pleasure — they plunge into a foul hell.”
धेरै विचारले भ्रमित, मोहको जालमा बेरिएका, इन्द्रियसुखमा समर्पित तिनीहरू नराम्रो नरकमा डुब्छन्।
अनेक विचारों से भ्रमित, मोह के जाल में लिपटे, इन्द्रिय-सुख में समर्पित वे घोर नरक में गिरते हैं।
16.17 ↗
आत्मसम्भाविताः स्तब्धा धनमानमदान्विताः । यजन्ते नामयज्ञैस्ते दम्भेनाविधिपूर्वकम्
ātmasambhāvitāḥ stabdhā dhanamānamadānvitāḥ । yajante nāmayajñaiste dambhenāvidhipūrvakam
Self-conceited, stubborn, filled with the pride of wealth, they perform sacrifices in name only, with hypocrisy, not according to the rule.“Self-important and obstinate, drunk with the pride of riches, they offer sacrifices only in show — hypocritical, not as scripture bids.”
आत्माभिमानी, जिद्दी, धनको घमण्डले भरिएका, तिनीहरू केवल नामका निम्ति, दम्भले, विधिअनुसार नभई यज्ञ गर्छन्।
आत्म-श्लाघी, हठी, धन के मद से भरे, वे केवल नाम के लिए, दम्भ से, विधि के अनुसार नहीं, यज्ञ करते हैं।

Cast into Demoniac Wombs 16.18–16.20

Filled with ego and hatred of the Lord dwelling in all bodies, these cruel haters are cast again and again into demoniac wombs, sinking to the lowest state.

16.18The hatred of the Lord
Filled with ego, power, pride, desire and anger, hating me in their own and in others' bodies, these malicious beings —
16.19Cast again and again
— these cruel haters, the vilest of men, I cast again and again into demoniac wombs in the rounds of rebirth.
16.20The lowest state
Entering a demoniac womb, deluded birth after birth, never reaching me, they sink to the lowest state, O son of Kuntī.
Read the verses
16.18 ↗
अहङ्कारं बलं दर्पं कामं क्रोधं च संश्रिताः । मामात्मपरदेहेषु प्रद्विषन्तोऽभ्यसूयकाः
ahaṅkāraṃ balaṃ darpaṃ kāmaṃ krodhaṃ ca saṃśritāḥ । māmātmaparadeheṣu pradviṣanto’bhyasūyakāḥ
Filled with ego, power, pride, desire and anger, hating me in their own and in others' bodies, these malicious beings —“Full of ego, might, arrogance, lust, and rage, they despise me dwelling in their own and others' hearts —”
अहंकार, बल, घमण्ड, कामना र क्रोधले भरिएका, आफ्नो र अरूको शरीरमा बसेको मलाई घृणा गर्ने यी दुष्ट प्राणीहरू —
अहंकार, बल, दर्प, कामना और क्रोध से भरे, अपने और दूसरों के शरीरों में स्थित मुझसे द्वेष रखने वाले ये दुष्ट प्राणी —
16.19 ↗
तानहं द्विषतः क्रूरान्संसारेषु नराधमान् । क्षिपाम्यजस्रमशुभानासुरीष्वेव योनिषु
tānahaṃ dviṣataḥ krūrānsaṃsāreṣu narādhamān । kṣipāmyajasramaśubhānāsurīṣveva yoniṣu
— these cruel haters, the vilest of men, I cast again and again into demoniac wombs in the rounds of rebirth.“— these cruel, hateful, lowest of men I hurl into demonic wombs, again and again, through the cycles of rebirth.”
— यी क्रूर घृणा गर्ने, नराधमहरूलाई, म पुनर्जन्मको चक्रमा बारम्बार आसुरी योनिहरूमा हाल्छु।
— इन क्रूर द्वेषियों, नराधमों को, मैं पुनर्जन्म के चक्रों में बार-बार आसुरी योनियों में डालता हूँ।
16.20 ↗
आसुरीं योनिमापन्ना मूढा जन्मनि जन्मनि । मामप्राप्यैव कौन्तेय ततो यान्त्यधमां गतिम्
āsurīṃ yonimāpannā mūḍhā janmani janmani । māmaprāpyaiva kaunteya tato yāntyadhamāṃ gatim
Entering a demoniac womb, deluded birth after birth, never reaching me, they sink to the lowest state, O son of Kuntī.“Born into demonic wombs, deluded life after life, never attaining me, they fall to the lowest state.”
आसुरी योनिमा परेर, जन्म-जन्म मोहित भएर, कहिल्यै मकहाँ नपुगी, तिनीहरू सबैभन्दा नीच अवस्थामा डुब्छन्, हे कुन्तीपुत्र।
आसुरी योनि में पड़कर, जन्म-जन्म मोहित होकर, कभी मुझे न पाकर, वे निम्नतम अवस्था को प्राप्त होते हैं, हे कुन्तीपुत्र।

Desire, anger, greed: three gates of hell 16.21–16.22

Desire, anger and greed — the threefold gate of hell and ruin of the self — must be abandoned; freed from them, one attains the supreme goal.

16.21The three gates
Threefold is this gate of hell, the destroyer of the self: desire, anger and greed — therefore abandon these three.
16.22Freed from the gates
Freed from these three gates of darkness, O son of Kuntī, a man works for his own good and attains the supreme goal.
Read the verses
16.21 ↗
त्रिविधं नरकस्येदं द्वारं नाशनमात्मनः । कामः क्रोधस्तथा लोभस्तस्मादेतत्त्रयं त्यजेत्
trividhaṃ narakasyedaṃ dvāraṃ nāśanamātmanaḥ । kāmaḥ krodhastathā lobhastasmādetattrayaṃ tyajet
Threefold is this gate of hell, the destroyer of the self: desire, anger and greed — therefore abandon these three.“Three doors lead to hell and ruin the soul: lust, anger, and greed. Give up all three.”
आत्माको नाश गर्ने नरकको यो द्वार त्रिविध छ: कामना, क्रोध र लोभ — त्यसैले यी तीनलाई त्याग।
आत्मा का नाश करने वाला नरक का यह द्वार त्रिविध है: काम, क्रोध और लोभ — इसलिए इन तीनों को त्याग दे।
16.22 ↗
एतैर्विमुक्तः कौन्तेय तमोद्वारैस्त्रिभिर्नरः । आचरत्यात्मनः श्रेयस्ततो याति परां गतिम्
etairvimuktaḥ kaunteya tamodvāraistribhirnaraḥ । ācaratyātmanaḥ śreyastato yāti parāṃ gatim
Freed from these three gates of darkness, O son of Kuntī, a man works for his own good and attains the supreme goal.“One who escapes these three dark gates, Arjuna, works for his own welfare and reaches the highest goal.”
हे कुन्तीपुत्र, अन्धकारका यी तीन ढोकाहरूबाट मुक्त भएर, मानिस आफ्नो हितका निम्ति कर्म गर्छ र परम लक्ष्यमा पुग्छ।
हे कुन्तीपुत्र, अन्धकार के इन तीन द्वारों से मुक्त होकर, मनुष्य अपने कल्याण के लिए कर्म करता है और परम लक्ष्य को प्राप्त होता है।

The Authority of Scripture 16.23–16.24

He who acts by the impulse of desire, casting scripture aside, gains neither perfection nor happiness; let scripture be the authority for what is to be done.

16.23Casting scripture aside
He who, casting aside the ordinance of scripture, acts by the impulse of desire — he attains neither perfection, nor happiness, nor the supreme goal.
16.24Let scripture be your guide
Therefore let the scripture be your authority in determining what is to be done and not done; knowing what is ordained by the scripture, act here accordingly.
Read the verses
16.23 ↗
यः शास्त्रविधिमुत्सृज्य वर्तते कामकारतः । न स सिद्धिमवाप्नोति न सुखं न परां गतिम्
yaḥ śāstravidhimutsṛjya vartate kāmakārataḥ । na sa siddhimavāpnoti na sukhaṃ na parāṃ gatim
He who, casting aside the ordinance of scripture, acts by the impulse of desire — he attains neither perfection, nor happiness, nor the supreme goal.“One who ignores the scripture's rule and acts on whim gains neither perfection, nor joy, nor the supreme end.”
जसले शास्त्रको विधानलाई थाती राखेर, कामनाको आवेगले कर्म गर्छ — ऊ न सिद्धि पाउँछ, न सुख, न परम लक्ष्य।
जो शास्त्र-विधान को त्यागकर, कामना के आवेग से कर्म करता है — वह न सिद्धि पाता है, न सुख, न परम लक्ष्य।
16.24 ↗
तस्माच्छास्त्रं प्रमाणं ते कार्याकार्यव्यवस्थितौ । ज्ञात्वा शास्त्रविधानोक्तं कर्म कर्तुमिहार्हसि
tasmācchāstraṃ pramāṇaṃ te kāryākāryavyavasthitau । jñātvā śāstravidhānoktaṃ karma kartumihārhasi
Therefore let the scripture be your authority in determining what is to be done and not done; knowing what is ordained by the scripture, act here accordingly.“So let scripture be your guide in deciding right and wrong; knowing its command, do your work here accordingly.”
त्यसैले के गर्नुपर्छ र के गर्नु हुँदैन भन्ने निर्णयमा शास्त्र नै तिम्रो प्रमाण होस्; शास्त्रले विधान गरेको जानेर, यहाँ त्यसैअनुसार कर्म गर।
इसलिए कर्तव्य-अकर्तव्य के निर्णय में शास्त्र ही तेरा प्रमाण हो; शास्त्र के विधान को जानकर, यहाँ उसी के अनुसार कर्म कर।
