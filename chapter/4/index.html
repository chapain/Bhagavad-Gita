This yoga is ancient, handed down4.1–4.3

Kṛṣṇa reveals the immemorial lineage of this yoga — from Vivasvān to Manu to Ikṣvāku — lost in time and now restored to Arjuna.

4.1Taught to vivasvān
I declared this imperishable yoga to Vivasvān; Vivasvān told it to Manu; Manu proclaimed it to Ikṣvāku.
4.2In time this yoga was lost
Handed down from teacher to student, the sage-kings knew it — but after a very long time it was lost, Arjuna.
4.3I declare that ancient yoga to you
That same ancient yoga I now teach to you, for you are devoted to me and you are my friend — this is the supreme secret.

श्रीभगवानुवाच । इमं विवस्वते योगं प्रोक्तवानहमव्ययम् । विवस्वान्मनवे प्राह मनुरिक्ष्वाकवेऽब्रवीत्
śrībhagavānuvāca । imaṃ vivasvate yogaṃ proktavānahamavyayam । vivasvānmanave prāha manurikṣvākave’bravīt
The Blessed Lord said: I declared this imperishable yoga to Vivasvān; Vivasvān communicated it to Manu; Manu proclaimed it to Ikṣvāku.Kṛṣṇa said: “Long ago I taught this eternal yoga to the sun-god Vivasvān; he passed it on to Manu, and Manu to King Ikṣvāku.”
श्रीभगवान्ले भन्नुभयो: यो अविनाशी योग मैले विवस्वान्लाई भनेको थिएँ; विवस्वान्ले मनुलाई भने; मनुले इक्ष्वाकुलाई प्रचार गरे।
श्री भगवान् ने कहा: इस अविनाशी योग को मैंने विवस्वान् से कहा; विवस्वान् ने मनु को बताया; मनु ने इक्ष्वाकु को कहा।

evaṃ paramparāprāptamimaṃ rājarṣayo viduḥ । sa kāleneha mahatā yogo naṣṭaḥ parantapa

यसरी परम्पराले ग्रहण गरेर राजर्षिहरूले यसलाई जाने; तर लामो समयको क्रममा, हे शत्रुतापन, यो योग लोप भयो।
इस प्रकार परम्परा से ग्रहण करके राजर्षियों ने इसे जाना; किन्तु दीर्घ काल के क्रम में, हे परंतप, यह योग लुप्त हो गया।

स एवायं मया तेऽद्य योगः प्रोक्तः पुरातनः । भक्तोऽसि मे सखा चेति रहस्यं ह्येतदुत्तमम्
sa evāyaṃ mayā te’dya yogaḥ proktaḥ purātanaḥ । bhakto’si me sakhā ceti rahasyaṃ hyetaduttamam

यही प्राचीन योग आज म तिमीलाई बताउँछु, किनकि तिमी मेरो भक्त र मित्र हौ — साँच्चै, यो परम गोप्य हो।
यही प्राचीन योग आज मैं तुझसे कहता हूँ, क्योंकि तू मेरा भक्त और मित्र है — सचमुच, यह परम गुह्य है।

Arjuna asks: how could you teach it then?4.4

How could you, born later, have taught this yoga to Vivasvān at the beginning?

4.4Arjuna asks how that can be
Your birth is later, Vivasvān's earlier — how am I to understand that you declared it in the beginning?

अर्जुन उवाच । अपरं भवतो जन्म परं जन्म विवस्वतः । कथमेतद्विजानीयां त्वमादौ प्रोक्तवानिति
arjuna uvāca । aparaṃ bhavato janma paraṃ janma vivasvataḥ । kathametadvijānīyāṃ tvamādau proktavāniti
Arjuna said: Later is your birth, and earlier the birth of Vivasvān; how then am I to understand that you taught it in the beginning?Arjuna asks: “You were born long after the sun-god Vivasvān. How could you have taught him this yoga at the very beginning?”
अर्जुनले भने: तिम्रो जन्म पछि, र विवस्वान्को जन्म पहिले; तब तिमीले सुरुमा यो सिकाएको थियौ भनी म कसरी बुझूँ?
अर्जुन ने कहा: तेरा जन्म पीछे है, और विवस्वान् का जन्म पहले; तो मैं कैसे समझूँ कि तूने आदि में इसे सिखाया था?

I take birth age after age4.5–4.9

Many births have passed for both of us; unborn though I am, I come into being by my own power whenever dharma declines.

4.5My births are divine
Many births of mine have passed, and of yours too; I know them all, but you do not know them.
4.6I come into being by my own māyā
I am unborn and eternal, the Lord of all beings — yet, taking up my own nature, I appear in the world through my own māyā.
4.7Whenever dharma declines
Whenever there is a decline of dharma and a rise of adharma, then I bring myself into being.
4.8To protect the righteous
To protect the righteous, to destroy the wicked, and to restore righteousness, I am born in every age.
4.9Knowing this, one is not reborn
He who truly knows my divine birth and work is not reborn on leaving the body — he comes to me.

śrībhagavānuvāca । bahūni me vyatītāni janmāni tava cārjuna । tānyahaṃ veda sarvāṇi na tvaṃ vettha parantapa
The Blessed Lord said: Many births of mine have passed, and of yours too, O Arjuna; I know them all, but you do not know them, O scorcher of foes.Kṛṣṇa said: “Both of us have passed through countless births, Arjuna. I remember them all; you do not.”
श्रीभगवान्ले भन्नुभयो: मेरा धेरै जन्महरू बितिसकेका छन्, हे अर्जुन, र तिम्रा पनि; म ती सबै जान्दछु, तर तिमी जान्दैनौ, हे शत्रुतापन।
श्री भगवान् ने कहा: मेरे अनेक जन्म बीत चुके हैं, हे अर्जुन, और तेरे भी; मैं उन सबको जानता हूँ, किन्तु तू नहीं जानता, हे परंतप।
4.6 ↗
अजोऽपि सन्नव्ययात्मा भूतानामीश्वरोऽपि सन् । प्रकृतिं स्वामधिष्ठाय सम्भवाम्यात्ममायया
ajo’pi sannavyayātmā bhūtānāmīśvaro’pi san । prakṛtiṃ svāmadhiṣṭhāya sambhavāmyātmamāyayā
Though unborn, though the imperishable Self, though the Lord of beings — resorting to my own prakṛti, I come into being through my own māyā.“I am unborn and eternal, the Lord of all beings — yet, taking up my own nature, I appear in the world through my own divine power.”
यद्यपि अजन्मा, अविनाशी आत्मा, प्राणीहरूको स्वामी — आफ्नै प्रकृतिलाई आश्रय गरेर, आफ्नै मायाद्वारा म उत्पन्न हुन्छु।
यद्यपि मैं अजन्मा हूँ, अविनाशी आत्मा हूँ, प्राणियों का स्वामी हूँ — अपनी प्रकृति का आश्रय लेकर, अपनी माया से मैं प्रकट होता हूँ।
4.7 ↗
यदा यदा हि धर्मस्य ग्लानिर्भवति भारत । अभ्युत्थानमधर्मस्य तदात्मानं सृजाम्यहम्
yadā yadā hi dharmasya glānirbhavati bhārata । abhyutthānamadharmasya tadātmānaṃ sṛjāmyaham
Whenever, O Bhārata, there is a decline of dharma and a rise of adharma, then I bring myself into being.“Whenever righteousness wanes and unrighteousness waxes, Arjuna, I manifest myself.”
हे भारत, जब-जब धर्मको ह्रास र अधर्मको वृद्धि हुन्छ, तब-तब म आफैंलाई प्रकट गर्छु।
हे भारत, जब-जब धर्म का ह्रास और अधर्म का उदय होता है, तब-तब मैं अपने आपको प्रकट करता हूँ।
4.8 ↗
परित्राणाय साधूनां विनाशाय च दुष्कृताम् । धर्मसंस्थापनार्थाय सम्भवामि युगे युगे
paritrāṇāya sādhūnāṃ vināśāya ca duṣkṛtām । dharmasaṃsthāpanārthāya sambhavāmi yuge yuge
For the protection of the good, for the destruction of the evil-doers, for the establishment of dharma, I take birth age after age.“To protect the righteous, to destroy the wicked, and to restore righteousness, I am born in every age.”
असलहरूको रक्षा, दुष्टहरूको विनाश, र धर्मको स्थापनाका निम्ति म हरेक युगमा जन्म लिन्छु।
साधुओं की रक्षा, दुष्टों का विनाश, और धर्म की स्थापना के लिए मैं युग-युग में जन्म लेता हूँ।
4.9 ↗
जन्म कर्म च मे दिव्यमेवं यो वेत्ति तत्त्वतः । त्यक्त्वा देहं पुनर्जन्म नैति मामेति सोऽर्जुन
janma karma ca me divyamevaṃ yo vetti tattvataḥ । tyaktvā dehaṃ punarjanma naiti māmeti so’rjuna
He who thus knows in truth my divine birth and action — having abandoned the body, he is not born again; he comes to me, O Arjuna.“Whoever truly understands my divine birth and deeds is never reborn after death — he comes to me, Arjuna.”
जसले यसरी साँच्चै मेरो दिव्य जन्म र कर्म जान्दछ — शरीर त्यागेर ऊ फेरि जन्मँदैन; ऊ मकहाँ आउँछ, हे अर्जुन।
जो इस प्रकार सचमुच मेरे दिव्य जन्म और कर्म को जानता है — शरीर त्यागकर वह फिर जन्म नहीं लेता; वह मुझे प्राप्त होता है, हे अर्जुन।

The Way of Approach4.10–4.12

Freed from passion and fear, many have reached me; and in whatever way men approach me, in that way I receive them.

4.10Freed from passion and fear
Freed from attachment, fear and anger, absorbed in me, purified by the austerity of knowledge, many have attained my being.
4.11As they approach, so I receive
However you come to me, I receive you in that way. Every path leads back to me, Arjuna.
4.12Those who want quick fruit worship gods
People seeking quick results worship the deities; in the human world, results do come quickly from action.
Read the verses
4.10 ↗
वीतरागभयक्रोधा मन्मया मामुपाश्रिताः । बहवो ज्ञानतपसा पूता मद्भावमागताः
vītarāgabhayakrodhā manmayā māmupāśritāḥ । bahavo jñānatapasā pūtā madbhāvamāgatāḥ
Free from attachment, fear and anger, filled with me, taking refuge in me, many — purified by the austerity of knowledge — have attained my state.“Those free of craving, fear and anger, who take shelter in me, are purified by the fire of wisdom and reach my very being.”
आसक्ति, भय र क्रोधबाट मुक्त, ममा भरिएका, मेरो शरण लिने धेरै — ज्ञानरूपी तपस्या (तप)ले शुद्ध भएर — मेरो अवस्था प्राप्त गरेका छन्।
आसक्ति, भय और क्रोध से मुक्त, मुझमें मग्न, मेरी शरण लेने वाले अनेकों — ज्ञान-तप से शुद्ध होकर — मेरी अवस्था को प्राप्त हुए हैं।

ये यथा मां प्रपद्यन्ते तांस्तथैव भजाम्यहम् । मम वर्त्मानुवर्तन्ते मनुष्याः पार्थ सर्वशः
ye yathā māṃ prapadyante tāṃstathaiva bhajāmyaham । mama vartmānuvartante manuṣyāḥ pārtha sarvaśaḥ

मनुष्य जिस-जिस प्रकार मुझे प्राप्त होते हैं, उसी-उसी प्रकार मैं उनका सत्कार करता हूँ; हे पार्थ, लोग सब प्रकार से मेरे ही मार्ग का अनुसरण करते हैं।

काङ्क्षन्तः कर्मणां सिद्धिं यजन्त इह देवताः । क्षिप्रं हि मानुषे लोके सिद्धिर्भवति कर्मजा
kāṅkṣantaḥ karmaṇāṃ siddhiṃ yajanta iha devatāḥ । kṣipraṃ hi mānuṣe loke siddhirbhavati karmajā

यस संसारमा कर्मको सिद्धि चाहनेहरू देवताहरूको पूजा गर्छन्; किनकि मानव लोकमा कर्मजन्य सिद्धि छिट्टै आउँछ।
इस लोक में कर्म की सिद्धि चाहने वाले देवताओं की पूजा करते हैं; क्योंकि मनुष्य-लोक में कर्म-जन्य सिद्धि शीघ्र मिलती है।

I am the author, yet not the doer4.13–4.15

The fourfold order was created by me according to guṇa and action — yet know me, its author, as the eternal non-doer.

4.13The fourfold order was created by me
The fourfold order was created by me according to the distribution of guṇas and actions; though its author, know me as the non-doer, changeless.
4.14Actions do not taint me
I crave no results, so actions never touch me. Whoever understands this is equally free.
4.15The ancients acted so
The wise of old, longing for freedom, acted this way. You too should act, Arjuna, as they did before you.

4.13 ↗
चातुर्वर्ण्यं मया सृष्टं गुणकर्मविभागशः । तस्य कर्तारमपि मां विद्ध्यकर्तारमव्ययम्
cāturvarṇyaṃ mayā sṛṣṭaṃ guṇakarmavibhāgaśaḥ । tasya kartāramapi māṃ viddhyakartāramavyayam
The fourfold order was created by me according to the distinction of guṇas and actions; though I am its author, know me as the non-doer and imperishable.“I created the four orders of society by qualities and work — yet I stand beyond them all, the changeless non-doer.”
गुण र कर्मको विभागअनुसार मैले चार वर्ण सृष्टि गरेको हुँ; यद्यपि म यसको कर्ता हुँ, मलाई अकर्ता र अविनाशी जान।
गुण और कर्म के विभाग के अनुसार मैंने चार वर्णों की रचना की है; यद्यपि मैं इसका कर्ता हूँ, मुझे अकर्ता और अविनाशी जान।

न मां कर्माणि लिम्पन्ति न मे कर्मफले स्पृहा । इति मां योऽभिजानाति कर्मभिर्न स बध्यते
na māṃ karmāṇi limpanti na me karmaphale spṛhā । iti māṃ yo’bhijānāti karmabhirna sa badhyate

कर्मले मलाई लाग्दैन, न मलाई कर्मफलको कामना नै छ; जसले मलाई यसरी जान्दछ, ऊ पनि कर्मले बाँधिँदैन।
कर्म मुझे लिप्त नहीं करते, न मुझे कर्म-फल की इच्छा ही है; जो मुझे ऐसा जानता है, वह भी कर्मों से नहीं बँधता।
4.15 ↗
एवं ज्ञात्वा कृतं कर्म पूर्वैरपि मुमुक्षुभिः । कुरु कर्मैव तस्मात्त्वं पूर्वैः पूर्वतरं कृतम्

यह जानकर प्राचीन मुक्तिकामियों ने कर्म किया; इसलिए तू भी कर्म कर, जैसा पूर्वजों ने किया था।

What is action, what is inaction?4.16–4.18

The riddle at the heart of karma-yoga: even the wise are bewildered about what action and inaction are.

4.16Even poets are confounded
What is action, what is inaction? Even the wise are bewildered here. The nature of action, of forbidden action and of inaction must be understood — mysterious is the course of action.
4.17The mystery of action
You must understand action, wrong action, and non-action — the ways of karma are deep and hard to fathom.
4.18The non-doer sees
He who sees inaction in action, and action in inaction, is wise among men; he is engaged in yoga and performs all actions.

Though he acts, he does nothing4.19–4.23

The portrait of the man whose works are burnt by knowledge: content with what comes unsought, beyond the pairs, and his actions dissolve entirely.

4.19Desires burnt by knowledge
Whose undertakings are all free from desire and selfish intention, and whose actions are burnt by the fire of knowledge — him the wise call a man of learning.
4.20Free from fever
Free from clinging to results, always satisfied, needing nothing — such a person acts, yet nothing is done by him.
4.21Owning nothing, he incurs no sin
Desireless, self-controlled, owning nothing, he acts only with the body — and no sin touches him.
4.22Content with what comes
Happy with whatever comes, beyond heat and cold, win and loss, free of jealousy — his actions never bind him.
4.23The freed man's action
One who is unattached and free, whose mind rests in wisdom and whose work is an offering — his karma dissolves entirely.

4.29Breath offered to breath

4.30Breath offered into breath, sins consumed
Others, eating little, offer breath into breath. All who understand sacrifice have their sins consumed by sacrifice.

evaṃ bahuvidhā yajñā vitatā brahmaṇo mukhe । karmajānviddhi tānsarvānevaṃ jñātvā vimokṣyase
Thus manifold sacrifices are set forth in the scriptures; know them all as born of action — knowing this, you shall be released.“The scriptures describe many kinds of sacrifice. Know that all arise from action — and understanding this sets you free.”
यसरी शास्त्रमा धेरै प्रकारका यज्ञहरू बताइएका छन्; ती सबै कर्मबाट जन्मेका हुन् भनी जान — यो जानेर तिमी मुक्त हुनेछौ।
इस प्रकार शास्त्र में अनेक प्रकार के यज्ञ बताए गए हैं; उन सबको कर्म से उत्पन्न जान — यह जानकर तू मुक्त हो जाएगा।
4.33 ↗
श्रेयान्द्रव्यमयाद्यज्ञाज्ज्ञानयज्ञः परन्तप । सर्वं कर्माखिलं पार्थ ज्ञाने परिसमाप्यते
śreyāndravyamayādyajñājjñānayajñaḥ parantapa । sarvaṃ karmākhilaṃ pārtha jñāne parisamāpyate
The sacrifice of knowledge is better than the sacrifice of material things, O scorcher of foes; all action, in its entirety, O Pārtha, culminates in knowledge.“The offering of wisdom is greater than any material offering — all action, without exception, reaches its end in knowledge.”
हे शत्रुतापन, द्रव्यमय यज्ञभन्दा ज्ञानयज्ञ श्रेष्ठ छ; हे पार्थ, सम्पूर्ण कर्म ज्ञानमै पर्यवसान हुन्छ।
हे परंतप, द्रव्यमय यज्ञ से ज्ञान-यज्ञ श्रेष्ठ है; हे पार्थ, सम्पूर्ण कर्म ज्ञान में ही पर्यवसित होता है।

Go to the wise and ask4.34–4.35

How knowledge is got: by prostration, by questioning, by service. Those who have seen the truth will teach it — and knowing it you will never be deluded again.

4.34Approach a teacher, ask, serve
Know this by prostration, by questioning, by service; the wise who have seen the truth will teach you that knowledge — knowing which you will not fall into delusion again, and will see all beings in the Self and in me.
4.35The better path
With this knowledge you will never be deluded again — you will see all beings in yourself, and in me.
Read the verses
4.34 ↗
तद्विद्धि प्रणिपातेन परिप्रश्नेन सेवया । उपदेक्ष्यन्ति ते ज्ञानं ज्ञानिनस्तत्त्वदर्शिनः
tadviddhi praṇipātena paripraśnena sevayā । upadekṣyanti te jñānaṃ jñāninastattvadarśinaḥ
Know that wisdom through prostration, by questioning, and by service; the knowers of truth who have seen reality will instruct you.“Approach the wise with humility, ask sincerely, and serve them — the seers of truth will impart wisdom to you.”
तत्त्वज्ञानलाई प्रणिपात, प्रश्न र सेवाद्वारा जान; सत्य देखेका तत्त्वदर्शीहरूले तिमीलाई उपदेश गर्नेछन्।
उस तत्त्व-ज्ञान को प्रणिपात, प्रश्न और सेवा से जान; सत्य को देख चुके तत्त्वदर्शी तुझे उपदेश देंगे।
4.35 ↗
यज्ज्ञात्वा न पुनर्मोहमेवं यास्यसि पाण्डव । येन भूतान्यशेषेण द्रक्ष्यस्यात्मन्यथो मयि
yajjñātvā na punarmohamevaṃ yāsyasi pāṇḍava । yena bhūtānyaśeṣeṇa drakṣyasyātmanyatho mayi
Knowing that, O son of Pāṇḍu, you will not again fall under delusion; by it you will see all beings without exception in the Self, and also in me.“With this knowledge you will never be deluded again — you will see all beings in yourself, and in me.”
त्यो जानेर, हे पाण्डुपुत्र, तिमी फेरि मोहमा पर्नेछैनौ; यसद्वारा तिमी सबै प्राणीलाई आफूमा र ममा देख्नेछौ।
उसे जानकर, हे पाण्डुपुत्र, तू फिर मोह में नहीं पड़ेगा; उससे तू सब प्राणियों को अपने में और मुझमें देखेगा।

Knowledge burns all actions to ashes4.36–4.41

The praise of jñāna: it carries the worst sinner across, burns karma as fire burns fuel, and nothing here purifies like it — but the doubter, faithless, perishes.

4.36Faith removes doubt
Even if you were the worst sinner imaginable, the boat of knowledge would carry you across all your sins.
4.37The fire of wisdom
Just as fire burns fuel to ash, the fire of wisdom burns up all karma.
4.38The peace of knowledge
Nothing purifies like wisdom. The yogi who is ready finds it arising within himself, in due time.
4.39The doubt perishes
One who has faith, devotion to the goal, and mastery of the senses gains wisdom — and with wisdom, he soon reaches supreme peace.
4.40The doubting soul perishes
The ignorant and faithless, torn by doubt, are lost — the doubter enjoys nothing here or beyond.
4.41Yoga burns the doubt
One who has surrendered his karma through yoga, whose doubts are cut by wisdom — nothing can bind him, Arjuna.
Read the verses
4.36 ↗
अपि चेदसि पापेभ्यः सर्वेभ्यः पापकृत्तमः । सर्वं ज्ञानप्लवेनैव वृजिनं सन्तरिष्यसि
api cedasi pāpebhyaḥ sarvebhyaḥ pāpakṛttamaḥ । sarvaṃ jñānaplavenaiva vṛjinaṃ santariṣyasi
Even if you are the most sinful of all sinners, you shall cross all evil by the boat of knowledge alone.“Even if you were the worst sinner imaginable, the boat of knowledge would carry you across all your sins.”
यदि तिमी सबै पापीहरूमा सबैभन्दा पापी पनि भएँ भने, ज्ञानरूपी डुङ्गाले नै तिमी सबै पाप पार गर्नेछौ।
यदि तू सब पापियों में सबसे बड़ा पापी भी हो, तो भी तू ज्ञान-रूप नाव से ही सब पापों को पार कर जाएगा।
4.37 ↗
यथैधांसि समिद्धोऽग्निर्भस्मसात्कुरुतेऽर्जुन । ज्ञानाग्निः सर्वकर्माणि भस्मसात्कुरुते तथा
yathaidhāṃsi samiddho’gnirbhasmasātkurute’rjuna । jñānāgniḥ sarvakarmāṇi bhasmasātkurute tathā
As the kindled fire reduces fuel to ashes, O Arjuna, so does the fire of knowledge reduce all actions to ashes.“Just as fire burns fuel to ash, the fire of wisdom burns up all karma.”
जसरी प्रज्वलित आगोले इन्धनलाई खरानी बनाउँछ, हे अर्जुन, त्यसरी नै ज्ञानाग्निले सबै कर्मलाई खरानी बनाउँछ।
जैसे प्रज्वलित अग्नि ईंधन को भस्म कर देती है, हे अर्जुन, वैसे ही ज्ञान-अग्नि सब कर्मों को भस्म कर देती है।
4.38 ↗
न हि ज्ञानेन सदृशं पवित्रमिह विद्यते । तत्स्वयं योगसंसिद्धः कालेनात्मनि विन्दति
na hi jñānena sadṛśaṃ pavitramiha vidyate । tatsvayaṃ yogasaṃsiddhaḥ kālenātmani vindati
Indeed, nothing here is as purifying as knowledge; one perfected in yoga finds it in the Self in time, of himself.“Nothing purifies like wisdom. The yogi who is ready finds it arising within himself, in due time.”
निश्चय नै यहाँ ज्ञानजस्तो पवित्र गर्ने अरू केही छैन; समयमा, योगमा सिद्ध व्यक्तिले यसलाई आफैं, आफ्नै आत्मामा पाउँछ।
निश्चय ही यहाँ ज्ञान के समान पवित्र करने वाला और कुछ नहीं है; योग में सिद्ध व्यक्ति उसे समय पर स्वयं, अपनी आत्मा में पाता है।
4.39 ↗
श्रद्धावांल्लभते ज्ञानं तत्परः संयतेन्द्रियः । ज्ञानं लब्ध्वा परां शान्तिमचिरेणाधिगच्छति
śraddhāvāṃllabhate jñānaṃ tatparaḥ saṃyatendriyaḥ । jñānaṃ labdhvā parāṃ śāntimacireṇādhigacchati
The faithful man, intent on it, with senses subdued, attains knowledge; and having attained knowledge, he soon reaches supreme peace.“One who has faith, devotion to the goal, and mastery of the senses gains wisdom — and with wisdom, deep and lasting peace.”
श्रद्धावान्, तत्पर, इन्द्रिय संयत व्यक्तिले ज्ञान प्राप्त गर्छ; र ज्ञान पाएर ऊ छिट्टै परम शान्तिमा पुग्छ।
श्रद्धावान, तत्पर, इन्द्रियों को वश में करने वाला पुरुष ज्ञान प्राप्त करता है; और ज्ञान प्राप्त करके वह शीघ्र ही परम शान्ति को प्राप्त होता है।
4.40 ↗
अज्ञश्चाश्रद्दधानश्च संशयात्मा विनश्यति । नायं लोकोऽस्ति न परो न सुखं संशयात्मनः
ajñaścāśraddadhānaśca saṃśayātmā vinaśyati । nāyaṃ loko’sti na paro na sukhaṃ saṃśayātmanaḥ
But the ignorant man without faith, full of doubt, perishes; for the doubting soul there is neither this world, nor the next, nor happiness.“The ignorant and faithless, torn by doubt, are lost — the doubter enjoys nothing here or beyond.”
तर अज्ञानी, श्रद्धारहित, सन्देहमा डुबेको मानिस नष्ट हुन्छ; सन्देही आत्माका निम्ति न यो लोक छ, न परलोक, न सुख।
किन्तु अज्ञानी, श्रद्धा-रहित, सन्देह से भरा हुआ पुरुष नष्ट होता है; सन्देही आत्मा के लिए न यह लोक है, न परलोक, न सुख।
4.41 ↗
योगसन्न्यस्तकर्माणं ज्ञानसञ्छिन्नसंशयम् । आत्मवन्तं न कर्माणि निबध्नन्ति धनञ्जय
yogasannyastakarmāṇaṃ jñānasañchinnasaṃśayam । ātmavantaṃ na karmāṇi nibadhnanti dhanañjaya
Actions do not bind him who has renounced actions through yoga, whose doubt is destroyed by knowledge, who is self-possessed, O Dhanañjaya.“One who has surrendered his karma through yoga, whose doubts are cut by wisdom — nothing can bind him, Arjuna.”
योगद्वारा कर्म त्यागेको, ज्ञानले सन्देह नष्ट भएको, आत्मायुक्त व्यक्तिलाई कर्मले बाँध्दैन, हे धनञ्जय।
योग द्वारा कर्मों का त्याग करने वाले, ज्ञान से सन्देह को नष्ट करने वाले, आत्मा में स्थित पुरुष को कर्म बाँधते नहीं, हे धनंजय।

Cut your doubt and arise4.42

The chapter's closing command, in two imperatives: cut the doubt lodged in your heart with the sword of knowledge, take your stand in yoga — and rise.

4.42Cut the doubt and take your stand
So cut the doubt in your heart with the sword of wisdom, take your stand in yoga — and rise up, Arjuna!
Read the verses
4.42 ↗
तस्मादज्ञानसम्भूतं हृत्स्थं ज्ञानासिनात्मनः । छित्त्वैनं संशयं योगमातिष्ठोत्तिष्ठ भारत
tasmādajñānasambhūtaṃ hṛtsthaṃ jñānāsinātmanaḥ । chittvainaṃ saṃśayaṃ yogamātiṣṭhottiṣṭha bhārata
Therefore, cutting with the sword of knowledge this doubt born of ignorance that dwells in your heart, stand firm in yoga — arise, O Bhārata!“So cut the doubt in your heart with the sword of wisdom, take your stand in yoga — and rise up, Arjuna!”
त्यसैले, हृदयमा बसेको अज्ञानजन्य यस सन्देहलाई ज्ञानरूपी तरबारले काटेर, योगमा दृढ भएर उठ, हे भारत!
इसलिए हृदय में स्थित अज्ञान-जन्य इस सन्देह को ज्ञान-रूप तलवार से काटकर, योग में स्थिर होकर उठ, हे भारत!
