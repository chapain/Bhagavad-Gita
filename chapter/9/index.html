The royal secret of all knowledge9.1–9.3

Kṛṣṇa reveals the greatest secret: knowledge with realization, the royal knowledge, which frees one from evil.

9.1The supreme secret
I will declare to you — who do not carp — this profoundest secret: knowledge with realization, knowing which you are freed from evil.
9.2The royal knowledge
This is the royal knowledge, the royal secret, the purest, directly realized, righteous, easy to practise, imperishable.
9.3The faithless return to death and rebirth
Those without faith in this teaching turn back on the path of death and rebirth, never reaching me.

Knowing it, you will be free of all evil.”

राजविद्या राजगुह्यं पवित्रमिदमुत्तमम् । प्रत्यक्षावगमं धर्म्यं सुसुखं कर्तुमव्ययम्
rājavidyā rājaguhyaṃ pavitramidamuttamam । pratyakṣāvagamaṃ dharmyaṃ susukhaṃ kartumavyayam

यो राजविद्या हो, राजगुह्य हो, परम पवित्र, प्रत्यक्ष अनुभवयोग्य, धर्मयुक्त, अभ्यास गर्न धेरै सजिलो र अविनाशी हो।
यह राज-विद्या है, राज-गुह्य है, परम पवित्र, प्रत्यक्ष अनुभव-योग्य, धर्म-युक्त, आचरण में अत्यन्त सरल और अविनाशी है।

इस धर्म में श्रद्धा न रखने वाले पुरुष, हे परंतप, मुझे प्राप्त न होकर, मृत्यु और पुनर्जन्म के मार्ग में लौट जाते हैं।

The Lord Who Pervades All9.4–9.6

All this is pervaded by me, yet I am not in them; the Lord upholds beings while remaining apart — by his yoga-māyā.

9.4All pervaded by me
I pervade this whole world in my unmanifest form; all beings dwell in me, yet I am not in them.
9.5The divine Yoga
Beings are not in me — behold my divine yoga! I sustain beings yet stand apart, like the vast wind moving everywhere in space.
9.6As wind in space
As the great wind moves everywhere and yet lives in space, so all beings live in me — understand this.

मया ततमिदं सर्वं जगदव्यक्तमूर्तिना । मत्स्थानि सर्वभूतानि न चाहं तेष्ववस्थितः
mayā tatamidaṃ sarvaṃ jagadavyaktamūrtinā । matsthāni sarvabhūtāni na cāhaṃ teṣvavasthitaḥ
By me this whole world is pervaded in my unmanifest form; all beings rest in me, but I am not contained in them.“I pervade this entire world in my unmanifest form. All beings dwell in me, yet I do not dwell in them.”
मेरो अव्यक्त रूपले यो सम्पूर्ण संसार मद्वारा व्याप्त छ; सबै प्राणीहरू ममा बस्छन्, तर म तिनमा समाहित छैन।
मेरे अव्यक्त रूप से यह सम्पूर्ण जगत् मुझ से व्याप्त है; सब प्राणी मुझ में रहते हैं, किन्तु मैं उनमें समाहित नहीं हूँ।

na ca matsthāni bhūtāni paśya me yogamaiśvaram । bhūtabhṛnna ca bhūtastho mamātmā bhūtabhāvanaḥ
And beings do not rest in me — behold my divine yoga! I sustain all beings yet stand apart, and my self is the origin of beings.“Beings do not truly rest in me — see my divine power! I support all beings, yet remain separate; I am their source.”
र प्राणीहरू ममा बस्दैनन् — मेरो दिव्य योग हेर! म सबै प्राणीलाई धान्छु तर अलग रहन्छु, र मेरो आत्मा नै प्राणीहरूको स्रोत हो।
और प्राणी मुझ में नहीं रहते — मेरी दिव्य योग-शक्ति देख! मैं सब प्राणियों को धारण करता हूँ फिर भी अलग रहता हूँ, और मेरी आत्मा ही प्राणियों का उत्पत्ति-स्थान है।

यथाकाशस्थितो नित्यं वायुः सर्वत्रगो महान् । तथा सर्वाणि भूतानि मत्स्थानीत्युपधारय
yathākāśasthito nityaṃ vāyuḥ sarvatrago mahān । tathā sarvāṇi bhūtāni matsthānītyupadhāraya
As the vast wind, ever moving, abides in space, so all beings abide in me — know this.“As the great wind moves everywhere and yet lives in space, so all beings live in me — understand this.”
जसरी ठूलो, सधैं चलायमान हावा आकाशमा रहन्छ, त्यसरी नै सबै प्राणीहरू ममा रहन्छन् — यो जान।
जैसे महान्, सदा चलने वाली वायु आकाश में रहती है, वैसे ही सब प्राणी मुझ में रहते हैं — यह जान।

The Cycles of Creation9.7–9.10

At the end of a kalpa all beings dissolve into my prakṛti; at the beginning I send them forth again — yet these actions do not bind me.

9.7The revolving creation
At the end of the cycle all beings return to my nature; at the beginning I send them forth again, helpless under nature's sway.
9.8Again and again I create
Through my own nature I project this entire multitude of beings again and again — creatures helpless under the power of nature.
9.9Unbound by action
These actions do not bind me, sitting indifferent, unattached; by my supervision nature brings forth the moving and unmoving.
9.10Under my direction
Under my direction nature produces everything moving and still; that is why the world turns.

9.7 ↗
सर्वभूतानि कौन्तेय प्रकृतिं यान्ति मामिकाम् । कल्पक्षये पुनस्तानि कल्पादौ विसृजाम्यहम्
sarvabhūtāni kaunteya prakṛtiṃ yānti māmikām । kalpakṣaye punastāni kalpādau visṛjāmyaham
All beings, O son of Kuntī, return to my prakṛti at the end of a kalpa; at the beginning of a kalpa I send them forth again.“At the end of the cosmic age, all beings merge back into my nature; at the dawn of the age, I send them out again.”
हे कुन्तीपुत्र, कल्पको अन्त्यमा सबै प्राणीहरू मेरो प्रकृतिमा फर्कन्छन्; कल्पको सुरुमा म तिनलाई फेरि बाहिर पठाउँछु।
हे कुन्तीपुत्र, कल्प के अन्त में सब प्राणी मेरी प्रकृति में लौट जाते हैं; कल्प के आरम्भ में मैं उन्हें फिर से उत्पन्न करता हूँ।
9.8 ↗
प्रकृतिं स्वामवष्टभ्य विसृजामि पुनः पुनः । भूतग्राममिमं कृत्स्नमवशं प्रकृतेर्वशात्
prakṛtiṃ svāmavaṣṭabhya visṛjāmi punaḥ punaḥ । bhūtagrāmamimaṃ kṛtsnamavaśaṃ prakṛtervaśāt
Taking hold of my own prakṛti, I send forth again and again this whole host of beings, helpless under the power of nature.“Through my own nature I project this entire multitude of beings again and again — creatures helpless under nature's sway.”
आफ्नै प्रकृतिलाई समातेर, म प्राणीहरूको यो सम्पूर्ण समूहलाई बारम्बार बाहिर पठाउँछु — प्रकृतिको शक्तिमा असहाय भएका।
अपनी ही प्रकृति को धारण करके, मैं इस सम्पूर्ण प्राणी-समूह को बार-बार उत्पन्न करता हूँ — प्रकृति की शक्ति में असहाय हुए।

न च मां तानि कर्माणि निबध्नन्ति धनञ्जय । उदासीनवदासीनमसक्तं तेषु कर्मसु
na ca māṃ tāni karmāṇi nibadhnanti dhanañjaya । udāsīnavadāsīnamasaktaṃ teṣu karmasu
These actions do not bind me, O Dhanañjaya, for I sit as one indifferent, unattached to these actions.“None of this binds me, Arjuna — I remain detached and indifferent, like an unconcerned onlooker.”
हे धनञ्जय, यी कर्महरूले मलाई बाँध्दैनन्, किनकि म तिनमा आसक्त नभई, उदासीन बसेको छु।
हे धनंजय, ये कर्म मुझे बाँधते नहीं, क्योंकि मैं इन कर्मों में आसक्त न होकर, उदासीन होकर बैठा हूँ।
9.10 ↗
मयाध्यक्षेण प्रकृतिः सूयते सचराचरम् । हेतुनानेन कौन्तेय जगद्विपरिवर्तते
mayādhyakṣeṇa prakṛtiḥ sūyate sacarācaram । hetunānena kaunteya jagadviparivartate
Under my supervision, prakṛti brings forth the moving and the unmoving; for this reason, O son of Kuntī, the world revolves.“Under my direction nature produces everything moving and still; that is why the world turns.”
मेरो अधीनमा, प्रकृतिले चर र अचर — सबै उत्पन्न गर्छे; यसै कारण, हे कुन्तीपुत्र, संसार घुमिरहन्छ।
मेरे अधीन रहकर प्रकृति चर और अचर — सबको उत्पन्न करती है; इसी कारण, हे कुन्तीपुत्र, संसार घूमता रहता है।

The Deluded and the Devoted9.11–9.15

The deluded, not knowing my supreme being, despise me in my human form; but the great souls, taking refuge in the divine nature, worship me.

9.11The deluded despise me
Fools, unaware of my supreme being, despise me wearing a human form; their hopes, works and knowledge are in vain.
9.12Their learning is in vain
Their hopes, deeds and learning are all in vain; confused and lost, they follow a deluded, demonic nature.
9.13The great souls know me as imperishable
But the great souls, resorting to the divine nature, worship me with undivided mind, knowing me as the imperishable source of beings.
9.14Ever glorifying me, firm in vows
Always glorifying me, striving with firm vows, bowing to me with devotion, ever united — they worship me.
9.15The sacrifice of knowledge
And others worship me with the sacrifice of knowledge — as the one, as the distinct, as the manifold facing everywhere.

I am the sacrifice and its goal9.16–9.19

I am the sacrifice, the oblation, the fire, the father, mother, support, goal, refuge and friend — I am everything.

9.16I am the sacrifice
I am the rite, the sacrifice, the oblation, the herb, the mantra, the butter, the fire and the offering.
9.17I am father and mother
I am the father and mother of the world, its support and grandsire; I am what is to be known, the purifier, the syllable Om, and the Ṛk, Sāma and Yajus.
9.18I am everything
I am the way, the lord, the witness, the abode, refuge and friend — the origin, dissolution, ground, treasure and eternal seed.
9.19I hold back the rain
I radiate warmth; I hold back the rain and release it. I am life and death, being and non-being, Arjuna.

The Finite Fruit of Works9.20–9.21

The ritualists who drink soma seek heaven and enjoy divine pleasures; but when merit is spent they return — desiring desires, they come and go.

9.20Soma-drinkers pray for the path to heaven
The Vedic scholars, drinking soma, purified of sin, worshipping by sacrifice, pray for heaven; they attain the world of Indra and enjoy celestial joys.
9.21Merit spent, they fall back to earth
When their merit is spent after enjoying vast heavenly bliss, they fall back into the mortal world — ever coming and going, desiring desires.

The Devoted Are My Care9.22–9.25

Those who worship me alone, ever united, I carry their yoga and kṣema; even those who worship other gods worship me — though wrongly.

To those ever united to me who worship me, I myself carry what they lack and preserve what they have.
9.23Even those who worship other gods

9.24I am the enjoyer of all sacrifices
For I alone am the enjoyer and lord of all sacrifices; not knowing me in truth, they fall. Worshippers of gods go to the gods, of ancestors to the ancestors — but my worshippers come to me.
9.25Each reaches the god he worships
God-worshippers reach the gods, ancestor-worshippers the ancestors, spirit-worshippers the spirits — but my worshippers come to me.

Offer every action to me; whatever is done with devotion, even a leaf or a drop, I accept; thus freed from bondage, you come to me.

9.28The fruit of renunciation
Doing this, you are freed from the ties of karma and their fruits of good and evil; renouncing in yoga, liberated, you shall come to me.

kṣipraṃ bhavati dharmātmā śaśvacchāntiṃ nigacchati । kaunteya pratijānīhi na me bhaktaḥ praṇaśyati
Quickly he becomes righteous of soul and attains lasting peace; know this for certain, O son of Kuntī: my devotee never perishes.“He soon becomes righteous and reaches enduring peace. Know it for certain: my devotee never comes to ruin.”
ऊ छिट्टै धर्मात्मा बन्छ र स्थायी शान्ति प्राप्त गर्छ; हे कुन्तीपुत्र, निश्चय जान: मेरो भक्त कहिल्यै नष्ट हुँदैन।
वह शीघ्र ही धर्मात्मा हो जाता है और स्थायी शान्ति को प्राप्त होता है; हे कुन्तीपुत्र, निश्चय जान: मेरा भक्त कभी नष्ट नहीं होता।

No one who takes refuge is excluded9.32–9.33

All who take refuge in me — even those of sinful birth, women, vaiśyas, śūdras — reach the supreme goal; how much more the holy brāhmaṇas and royal sages.

9.32All reach me
Those who take refuge in me — even those of sinful birth, women, traders and labourers — reach the supreme goal.
9.33Having come to this fleeting world, worship me
How much more then the holy brāhmaṇas and devoted royal sages! Having come to this impermanent, joyless world, worship me.
Read the verses
9.32 ↗
मां हि पार्थ व्यपाश्रित्य येऽपि स्युः पापयोनयः । स्त्रियो वैश्यास्तथा शूद्रास्तेऽपि यान्ति परां गतिम्
māṃ hi pārtha vyapāśritya ye’pi syuḥ pāpayonayaḥ । striyo vaiśyāstathā śūdrāste’pi yānti parāṃ gatim
For those who take refuge in me, O Pārtha — even those of sinful birth, women, traders and labourers — they too reach the supreme goal.“All who come to me for refuge — even the low-born, women, merchants, and workers — reach the highest goal.”
हे पार्थ, मेरो शरण लिनेहरू — पापी जन्मका, स्त्री, व्यापारी र मजदुरहरू पनि — ती पनि परम लक्ष्यमा पुग्छन्।
हे पार्थ, मेरी शरण लेने वाले — पाप-जन्म वाले, स्त्रियाँ, वैश्य और शूद्र भी — वे भी परम लक्ष्य को प्राप्त होते हैं।
9.33 ↗
किं पुनर्ब्राह्मणाः पुण्या भक्ता राजर्षयस्तथा । अनित्यमसुखं लोकमिमं प्राप्य भजस्व माम्
kiṃ punarbrāhmaṇāḥ puṇyā bhaktā rājarṣayastathā । anityamasukhaṃ lokamimaṃ prāpya bhajasva mām
How much more then the holy brāhmaṇas and the devoted royal sages! Having come to this impermanent, joyless world, worship me.“How much more the pure brāhmaṇas and devoted sage-kings! In this fleeting, sorrowful world, worship me.”
पवित्र ब्राह्मण र समर्पित राजर्षिहरूको त कुरै के! यस अनित्य, सुखरहित संसारमा आएर, मेरो पूजा गर।
तो फिर पवित्र ब्राह्मण और समर्पित राजर्षि — उनकी बात ही क्या! इस अनित्य, सुख-रहित संसार में आकर मेरी पूजा कर।

Think of me, bow to me, and come to me9.34–9.34

The chapter's closing instruction, given to Arjuna in four verbs — and the same verse Kṛṣṇa repeats at 18.65 as his final word.

9.34Fix your mind on me
Fix your thought on me, be my devotee, worship me, bow to me — devoted wholly to me, you will surely reach me.
Read the verses
9.34 ↗
मन्मना भव मद्भक्तो मद्याजी मां नमस्कुरु । मामेवैष्यसि युक्त्वैवमात्मानं मत्परायणः
manmanā bhava madbhakto madyājī māṃ namaskuru । māmevaiṣyasi yuktvaivamātmānaṃ matparāyaṇaḥ
Fix your mind on me, be my devotee, offer to me, bow to me — thus yoking the self to me, intent on me, you shall surely come to me.“Fix your thought on me, be my devotee, worship me, bow to me — devoted wholly to me, you will surely reach me.”
मन ममा लगाऊ, मेरो भक्त बन, मलाई अर्पण गर, मलाई नमस्कार गर — यसरी आत्मालाई ममा युक्त गरेर, ममै तत्पर भएर, तिमी निश्चय मकहाँ आउनेछौ।
मन मुझ में लगा, मेरा भक्त बन, मुझे अर्पण कर, मुझे प्रणाम कर — इस प्रकार आत्मा को मुझ में युक्त करके, मुझ में ही तत्पर होकर, तू निश्चय ही मुझे प्राप्त होगा।
