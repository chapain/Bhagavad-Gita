# Verse-by-verse padachheda review

## Scope and progress

**Boundary review complete: 1.01–18.78 (700/700 verses).**

**Outcome:** 20 fused-row occurrences split, two missing negatives restored,
six standalone-form corrections, affecting 27 verses. Final count **9,506**.
The boundary pass left printed Sanskrit and all literals/paraphrases unchanged.
**Subsequent source-backed conflict resolution:** [decisions, provenance and verification](padachheda-conflict-resolution.md).
That separate, owner-authorized pass resolves the collateral findings below,
including 11.47 Hindi, with corrections/clarifications in 18 verses and one
corroborated reading correction (6.19 yathā). The original batch entries below
are historical findings, not claims that those defects are still open. Neither
pass claims infallibility or a complete retranslation.

This is a sequential human/editorial read, not the earlier length-filtered worklist (`padachheda-review.md`). Every Sanskrit word-row boundary, including speaker rows and short entries, was read. Chapters 1–6 used en/ne/hi literal context; chapters 7–18 used the English literal for every verse, then en/ne/hi literals and five-field rows for proposed changes. New/changed glosses were read in all three languages. This is not a comprehensive retranslation or semantic certification of every unchanged gloss. Multiword English glosses alone do not imply multiword Sanskrit. Genuine compounds, names, verbal prefixes and derivational morphology are not split merely because they have identifiable parts.

This pass seeks missing word boundaries. Existing compound-component rows are not silently merged or redesigned. Nor is a boundary verdict a certification of every translation/gloss in that verse: collateral issues are explicitly recorded below. Literal translations remain unchanged and govern contextual meaning checks.
