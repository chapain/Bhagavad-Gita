अस्माकं तु विशिष्टा ये तान्निबोध द्विजोत्तम । नायका मम सैन्यस्य संज्ञार्थं तान्ब्रवीमि ते

But know, O best of the twice-born, those who are distinguished among us — the leaders of my army; I name them to you for your information.
