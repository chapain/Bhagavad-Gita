Bhagavad Gita 1.32 — open in the app →

न काङ्क्षे विजयं कृष्ण न च राज्यं सुखानि च । किं नो राज्येन गोविन्द किं भोगैर्जीवितेन वा

“I do not desire victory, O Kṛṣṇa, nor kingdom nor pleasures. Of what use is a kingdom to us, O Govinda — what use are enjoyments, or even life?”
