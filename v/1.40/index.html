कुलक्षये प्रणश्यन्ति कुलधर्माः सनातनाः । धर्मे नष्टे कुलं कृत्स्नमधर्मोऽभिभवत्युत

“With the destruction of the family, the eternal family-duties are lost; and when dharma is lost, adharma overwhelms the whole family.”
