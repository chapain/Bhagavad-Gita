दोषैरेतैः कुलघ्नानां वर्णसङ्करकारकैः । उत्साद्यन्ते जातिधर्माः कुलधर्माश्च शाश्वताः

“By these faults of the family-destroyers, which cause the mixing of classes, the eternal duties of the community and the family are destroyed.”
