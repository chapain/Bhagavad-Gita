Bhagavad Gita 11.7 — open in the app →

इहैकस्थं जगत्कृत्स्नं पश्याद्य सचराचरम् । मम देहे गुडाकेश यच्चान्यद्द्रष्टुमिच्छसि

Behold now the whole universe, moving and unmoving, gathered in one body here in mine, O Guḍākeśa — and whatever else you wish to see.
