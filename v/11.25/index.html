दंष्ट्राकरालानि च ते मुखानि दृष्ट्वैव कालानलसन्निभानि । दिशो न जाने न लभे च शर्म प्रसीद देवेश जगन्निवास

Seeing your terrible mouths with their fearful fangs, like the fire of the end of time, I do not know the directions, nor find any refuge; be gracious, O Lord of gods, abode of the world.
