Bhagavad Gita 11.29 — open in the app →

यथा प्रदीप्तं ज्वलनं पतङ्गा विशन्ति नाशाय समृद्धवेगाः । तथैव नाशाय विशन्ति लोकास्तवापि वक्त्राणि समृद्धवेगाः

As moths fly with full speed into a blazing fire for their destruction, so these worlds fly into your mouths for their destruction.
