Bhagavad Gita 11.31 — open in the app →

आख्याहि मे को भवानुग्ररूपो नमोऽस्तु ते देववर प्रसीद । विज्ञातुमिच्छामि भवन्तमाद्यं न हि प्रजानामि तव प्रवृत्तिम्

Tell me who you are, O terrible form; I bow to you — be gracious, O best of gods! I long to know you, the primeval one; I do not understand your workings.
