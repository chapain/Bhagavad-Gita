कस्माच्च ते न नमेरन्महात्मन्गरीयसे ब्रह्मणोऽप्यादिकर्त्रे । अनन्त देवेश जगन्निवास त्वमक्षरं सदसत्तत्परं यत्

Why should they not bow to you, O great soul, greater than Brahmā, the primal creator — O infinite Lord of gods, abode of the universe, you are the imperishable, being and non-being and beyond both.
