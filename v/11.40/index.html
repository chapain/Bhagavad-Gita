Bhagavad Gita 11.40 — open in the app →

नमः पुरस्तादथ पृष्ठतस्ते नमोऽस्तु ते सर्वत एव सर्व । अनन्तवीर्यामितविक्रमस्त्वं सर्वं समाप्नोषि ततोऽसि सर्वः

I bow to you from the front and from behind; I bow to you on every side, O all! You are of infinite might and measureless vigour; you pervade all, and so you are all.
