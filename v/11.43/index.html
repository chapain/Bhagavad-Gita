पितासि लोकस्य चराचरस्य त्वमस्य पूज्यश्च गुरुर्गरीयान् । न त्वत्समोऽस्त्यभ्यधिकः कुतोऽन्यो लोकत्रयेऽप्यप्रतिमप्रभाव

You are the father of this world, moving and unmoving; you are its supreme object of worship and the most venerable teacher; none equals you in the three worlds, how then could any surpass you, O being of matchless power?
