Bhagavad Gita 11.46 — open in the app →

किरीटिनं गदिनं चक्रहस्तमिच्छामि त्वां द्रष्टुमहं तथैव । तेनैव रूपेण चतुर्भुजेन सहस्रबाहो भव विश्वमूर्ते

I long to see you as before — crowned, with mace and discus in hand; appear in that four-armed form, O thousand-armed, universal form.
