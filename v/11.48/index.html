Bhagavad Gita 11.48 — open in the app →

न वेदयज्ञाध्ययनैर्न दानैर्न च क्रियाभिर्न तपोभिरुग्रैः । एवंरूपः शक्य अहं नृलोके द्रष्टुं त्वदन्येन कुरुप्रवीर

Not by the study of the Vedas and sacrifices, nor by gifts, rites or fierce austerities, can I be seen in this form in the world of men — by any but you, O prince of the Kurus.
