Bhagavad Gita 11.53 — open in the app →

नाहं वेदैर्न तपसा न दानेन न चेज्यया । शक्य एवंविधो द्रष्टुं दृष्टवानसि मां यथा

I am not to be seen in this way through the Vedas, nor through austerity, nor through gifts, nor through worship; only in the way you have seen me can I be seen.
