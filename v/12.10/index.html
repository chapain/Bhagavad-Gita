Bhagavad Gita 12.10 — open in the app →

अभ्यासेऽप्यसमर्थोऽसि मत्कर्मपरमो भव । मदर्थमपि कर्माणि कुर्वन्सिद्धिमवाप्स्यसि

If you are unable to practise even that, be devoted to working for me; even performing actions for my sake, you shall attain perfection.
