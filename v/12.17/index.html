Bhagavad Gita 12.17 — open in the app →

यो न हृष्यति न द्वेष्टि न शोचति न काङ्क्षति । शुभाशुभपरित्यागी भक्तिमान्यः स मे प्रियः

He who neither rejoices nor hates, neither grieves nor desires, renouncing good and evil — that devoted one is dear to me.
