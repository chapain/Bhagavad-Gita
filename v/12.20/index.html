Bhagavad Gita 12.20 — open in the app →

ये तु धर्म्यामृतमिदं यथोक्तं पर्युपासते । श्रद्दधाना मत्परमाः भक्तास्तेऽतीव मे प्रियाः

But those who follow this immortal dharma, as declared, with faith, making me the supreme goal — those devotees are exceedingly dear to me.
