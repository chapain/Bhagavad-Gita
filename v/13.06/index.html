Bhagavad Gita 13.6 — open in the app →

इच्छा द्वेषः सुखं दुःखं सङ्घातश्चेतना धृतिः । एतत्क्षेत्रं समासेन सविकारमुदाहृतम्

— desire, aversion, pleasure, pain, the body, consciousness and constancy: this is the field, in brief, with its modifications.
