Bhagavad Gita 13.11 — open in the app →

अध्यात्मज्ञाननित्यत्वं तत्त्वज्ञानार्थदर्शनम् । एतज्ज्ञानमिति प्रोक्तमज्ञानं यदतोऽन्यथा

— constant commitment to Self-knowledge, and insight into the purpose of the knowledge of truth: this is declared knowledge; what is other than this is ignorance.
