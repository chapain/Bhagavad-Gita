Bhagavad Gita 13.12 — open in the app →

ज्ञेयं यत्तत्प्रवक्ष्यामि यज्ज्ञात्वामृतमश्नुते । अनादिमत्परं ब्रह्म न सत्तन्नासदुच्यते

I shall declare the object of knowledge, knowing which one tastes immortality — the beginningless supreme Brahman, which is called neither being nor non-being.
