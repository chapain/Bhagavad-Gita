सर्वतःपाणिपादं तत्सर्वतोक्षिशिरोमुखम् । सर्वतःश्रुतिमल्लोके सर्वमावृत्य तिष्ठति

With hands and feet everywhere, with eyes, heads and faces everywhere, with ears everywhere — it stands, pervading all in the world.
