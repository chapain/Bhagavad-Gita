ज्योतिषामपि तज्ज्योतिस्तमसः परमुच्यते । ज्ञानं ज्ञेयं ज्ञानगम्यं हृदि सर्वस्य विष्ठितम्

It is the light of lights, declared beyond darkness — knowledge, the object of knowledge, and the goal of knowledge, seated in the heart of all.
