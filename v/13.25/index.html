Bhagavad Gita 13.25 — open in the app →

अन्ये त्वेवमजानन्तः श्रुत्वान्येभ्य उपासते । तेऽपि चातितरन्त्येव मृत्युं श्रुतिपरायणाः

Others, not knowing, worship after hearing from others; and they too cross beyond death, devoted to what they have heard.
