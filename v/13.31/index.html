Bhagavad Gita 13.31 — open in the app →

अनादित्वान्निर्गुणत्वात्परमात्मायमव्ययः । शरीरस्थोऽपि कौन्तेय न करोति न लिप्यते

Because it is beginningless and without guṇas, this supreme Self is imperishable — though dwelling in the body, it neither acts nor is stained.
