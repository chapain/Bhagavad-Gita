क्षेत्रक्षेत्रज्ञयोरेवमन्तरं ज्ञानचक्षुषा । भूतप्रकृतिमोक्षं च ये विदुर्यान्ति ते परम्

Those who, with the eye of knowledge, see the distinction between the field and its knower, and the liberation from prakṛti — they reach the supreme.
