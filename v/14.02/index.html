Bhagavad Gita 14.02 — open in the app →

इदं ज्ञानमुपाश्रित्य मम साधर्म्यमागताः । सर्गेऽपि नोपजायन्ते प्रलये न व्यथन्ति च

Taking refuge in this knowledge, they attain unity with my being; they are not born at creation, nor are they troubled at dissolution.
