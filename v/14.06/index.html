Bhagavad Gita 14.06 — open in the app →

तत्र सत्त्वं निर्मलत्वात्प्रकाशकमनामयम् । सुखसङ्गेन बध्नाति ज्ञानसङ्गेन चानघ

Of these, sattva, being pure, illumines and is free from disease; it binds by attachment to happiness and to knowledge, O sinless one.
