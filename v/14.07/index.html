Bhagavad Gita 14.7 — open in the app →

रजो रागात्मकं विद्धि तृष्णासङ्गसमुद्भवम् । तन्निबध्नाति कौन्तेय कर्मसङ्गेन देहिनम्

Know rajas as of the nature of passion, born of thirst and attachment; it binds the embodied one by attachment to action, O son of Kuntī.
