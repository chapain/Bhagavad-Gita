Bhagavad Gita 14.20 — open in the app →

गुणानेतानतीत्य त्रीन्देही देहसमुद्भवान् । जन्ममृत्युजरादुःखैर्विमुक्तोऽमृतमश्नुते

Transcending these three guṇas born of the body, freed from birth, death, old age and sorrow, the embodied one tastes immortality.
