समदुःखसुखः स्वस्थः समलोष्टाश्मकाञ्चनः । तुल्यप्रियाप्रियो धीरस्तुल्यनिन्दात्मसंस्तुतिः

— equal in pleasure and pain, self-abiding, to whom a clod, stone and gold are alike, equal to the pleasant and unpleasant, firm, equal in praise and blame —
