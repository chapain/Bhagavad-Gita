Bhagavad Gita 14.25 — open in the app →

मानापमानयोस्तुल्यस्तुल्यो मित्रारिपक्षयोः । सर्वारम्भपरित्यागी गुणातीतः स उच्यते

— equal in honour and dishonour, equal to friend and foe, renouncing all undertakings — he is said to have transcended the guṇas.
