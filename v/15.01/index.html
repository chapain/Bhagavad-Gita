श्रीभगवानुवाच । ऊर्ध्वमूलमधःशाखमश्वत्थं प्राहुरव्ययम् । छन्दांसि यस्य पर्णानि यस्तं वेद स वेदवित्

The Blessed Lord said: They speak of the imperishable aśvattha with its root above and branches below; the Vedas are its leaves — he who knows it is a knower of the Vedas.
