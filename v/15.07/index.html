Bhagavad Gita 15.7 — open in the app →

ममैवांशो जीवलोके जीवभूतः सनातनः । मनःषष्ठानीन्द्रियाणि प्रकृतिस्थानि कर्षति

An eternal portion of myself, become a living soul in the world of life, draws around itself the senses, of which the mind is the sixth, which abide in prakṛti.
