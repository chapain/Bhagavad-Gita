Bhagavad Gita 15.8 — open in the app →

शरीरं यदवाप्नोति यच्चाप्युत्क्रामतीश्वरः । गृहीत्वैतानि संयाति वायुर्गन्धानिवाशयात्

When the lord of the body takes up a body, and when he departs from it, he goes taking these with him, as the wind carries fragrance from its seat.
