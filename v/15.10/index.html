Bhagavad Gita 15.10 — open in the app →

उत्क्रामन्तं स्थितं वापि भुञ्जानं वा गुणान्वितम् । विमूढा नानुपश्यन्ति पश्यन्ति ज्ञानचक्षुषः

The deluded do not see him as he departs, dwells, or enjoys, or as he is joined with the guṇas; but those with the eye of knowledge see him.
