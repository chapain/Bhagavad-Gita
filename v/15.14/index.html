अहं वैश्वानरो भूत्वा प्राणिनां देहमाश्रितः । प्राणापानसमायुक्तः पचाम्यन्नं चतुर्विधम्

Becoming the fire of digestion in the bodies of living beings, joined with the in-breath and out-breath, I cook the four kinds of food.
