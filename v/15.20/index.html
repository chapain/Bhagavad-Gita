Bhagavad Gita 15.20 — open in the app →

इति गुह्यतमं शास्त्रमिदमुक्तं मयानघ । एतद्बुद्ध्वा बुद्धिमान्स्यात्कृतकृत्यश्च भारत

Thus this most secret teaching has been declared by me, O sinless one; understanding this, one becomes wise and has accomplished all, O Bhārata.
