Bhagavad Gita 16.3 — open in the app →

तेजः क्षमा धृतिः शौचमद्रोहो नातिमानिता । भवन्ति सम्पदं दैवीमभिजातस्य भारत

— vigour, forgiveness, fortitude, purity, freedom from malice and from pride — these belong to one born to the divine destiny, O Bhārata.
