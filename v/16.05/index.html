Bhagavad Gita 16.5 — open in the app →

दैवी सम्पद्विमोक्षाय निबन्धायासुरी मता । मा शुचः सम्पदं दैवीमभिजातोऽसि पाण्डव

The divine endowment leads to liberation, the demoniac to bondage — grieve not, O Pāṇḍava, for you are born to the divine destiny.
