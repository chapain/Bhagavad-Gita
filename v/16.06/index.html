Bhagavad Gita 16.06 — open in the app →

द्वौ भूतसर्गौ लोकेऽस्मिन्दैव आसुर एव च । दैवो विस्तरशः प्रोक्त आसुरं पार्थ मे शृणु

There are two creations of beings in this world — the divine and the demoniac; the divine has been described at length — now hear of the demoniac, O Pārtha.
