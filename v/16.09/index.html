Bhagavad Gita 16.09 — open in the app →

एतां दृष्टिमवष्टभ्य नष्टात्मानोऽल्पबुद्धयः । प्रभवन्त्युग्रकर्माणः क्षयाय जगतोऽहिताः

Holding this view, these ruined souls of little understanding perform cruel deeds, enemies of the world, for its destruction.
