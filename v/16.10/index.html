काममाश्रित्य दुष्पूरं दम्भमानमदान्विताः । मोहाद्गृहीत्वाऽसद्ग्राहान्प्रवर्तन्तेऽशुचिव्रताः

Given to insatiable desire, full of hypocrisy, pride and arrogance, holding false views through delusion, they act with impure resolves.
