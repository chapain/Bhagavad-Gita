Bhagavad Gita 16.15 — open in the app →

आढ्योऽभिजनवानस्मि कोऽन्योऽस्ति सदृशो मया । यक्ष्ये दास्यामि मोदिष्य इत्यज्ञानविमोहिताः

'I am rich and well-born; who else is like me? I will sacrifice, I will give, I will rejoice' — thus deluded by ignorance.
