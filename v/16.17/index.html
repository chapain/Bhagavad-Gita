आत्मसम्भाविताः स्तब्धा धनमानमदान्विताः । यजन्ते नामयज्ञैस्ते दम्भेनाविधिपूर्वकम्

Self-conceited, stubborn, filled with the pride of wealth, they perform sacrifices in name only, with hypocrisy, not according to the rule.
