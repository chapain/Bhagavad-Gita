Bhagavad Gita 16.23 — open in the app →

यः शास्त्रविधिमुत्सृज्य वर्तते कामकारतः । न स सिद्धिमवाप्नोति न सुखं न परां गतिम्

He who, casting aside the ordinance of scripture, acts by the impulse of desire — he attains neither perfection, nor happiness, nor the supreme goal.
