Bhagavad Gita 16.24 — open in the app →

तस्माच्छास्त्रं प्रमाणं ते कार्याकार्यव्यवस्थितौ । ज्ञात्वा शास्त्रविधानोक्तं कर्म कर्तुमिहार्हसि

Therefore let the scripture be your authority in determining what is to be done and not done; knowing what is ordained by the scripture, act here accordingly.
