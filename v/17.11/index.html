Bhagavad Gita 17.11 — open in the app →

अफलाकाङ्क्षिभिर्यज्ञो विधिदृष्टो य इज्यते । यष्टव्यमेवेति मनः समाधाय स सात्त्विकः

The sacrifice ordained by scripture, offered without desire for fruit by those who think 'this must be performed' — that is sāttvic.
