Bhagavad Gita 17.14 — open in the app →

देवद्विजगुरुप्राज्ञपूजनं शौचमार्जवम् । ब्रह्मचर्यमहिंसा च शारीरं तप उच्यते

Worship of the gods, the twice-born, teachers and the wise; purity, uprightness, chastity and non-violence — this is the austerity of the body.
