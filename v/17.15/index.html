Bhagavad Gita 17.15 — open in the app →

अनुद्वेगकरं वाक्यं सत्यं प्रियहितं च यत् । स्वाध्यायाभ्यसनं चैव वाङ्मयं तप उच्यते

Speech that causes no distress, that is truthful, pleasant and beneficial, and the regular study of scripture — this is the austerity of speech.
