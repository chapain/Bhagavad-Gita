Bhagavad Gita 17.20 — open in the app →

दातव्यमिति यद्दानं दीयतेऽनुपकारिणे । देशे काले च पात्रे च तद्दानं सात्त्विकं स्मृतम्

The gift given because it ought to be given, to one who can do nothing in return, at the right place and time, to a worthy person — is deemed sāttvic.
