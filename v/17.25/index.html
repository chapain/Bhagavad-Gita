तदित्यनभिसन्धाय फलं यज्ञतपःक्रियाः । दानक्रियाश्च विविधाः क्रियन्ते मोक्षकाङ्क्षिभिः

Uttering Tat, without desiring fruit, are the acts of sacrifice, austerity and the various acts of giving performed by seekers of liberation.
