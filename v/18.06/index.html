Bhagavad Gita 18.6 — open in the app →

एतान्यपि तु कर्माणि सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा फलानि च । कर्तव्यानीति मे पार्थ निश्चितं मतमुत्तमम्

But even these actions should be performed, giving up attachment and the fruits, O Pārtha — this is my certain and best conviction.
