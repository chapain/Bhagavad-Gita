Bhagavad Gita 18.11 — open in the app →

न हि देहभृता शक्यं त्यक्तुं कर्माण्यशेषतः । यस्तु कर्मफलत्यागी स त्यागीत्यभिधीयते

Indeed, the embodied one cannot give up all actions entirely; but he who gives up the fruit of action is called a true renouncer.
