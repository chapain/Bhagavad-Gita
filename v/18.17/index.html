Bhagavad Gita 18.17 — open in the app →

यस्य नाहंकृतो भावो बुद्धिर्यस्य न लिप्यते । हत्वापि स इमांल्लोकान्न हन्ति न निबध्यते

He whose mind is free from ego, whose intellect is not stained — though he slays these worlds, he slays not, nor is bound.
