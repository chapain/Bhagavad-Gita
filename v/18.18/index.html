Bhagavad Gita 18.18 — open in the app →

ज्ञानं ज्ञेयं परिज्ञाता त्रिविधा कर्मचोदना । करणं कर्म कर्तेति त्रिविधः कर्मसंग्रहः

Knowledge, the known and the knower — the threefold impulse to action; the instrument, the act and the agent — the threefold basis of action.
