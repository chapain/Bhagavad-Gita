Bhagavad Gita 18.19 — open in the app →

ज्ञानं कर्म च कर्ता च त्रिधैव गुणभेदतः । प्रोच्यते गुणसंख्याने यथावच्छृणु तान्यपि

Knowledge, action and agent are declared threefold by the distinction of the guṇas — hear them as they are in the enumeration of the guṇas.
