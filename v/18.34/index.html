Bhagavad Gita 18.34 — open in the app →

यया तु धर्मकामार्थान्धृत्या धारयतेऽर्जुन । प्रसङ्गेन फलाकाङ्क्षी धृतिः सा पार्थ राजसी

But the fortitude by which one holds to duty, pleasure and wealth, attached and desiring fruit — that, O Arjuna, is rājasic.
