Bhagavad Gita 18.36 — open in the app →

सुखं त्विदानीं त्रिविधं शृणु मे भरतर्षभ । अभ्यासाद्रमते यत्र दुःखान्तं च निगच्छति

Hear now from me, O bull of the Bhāratas, the threefold happiness — that in which one rejoices by practice and reaches the end of suffering.
