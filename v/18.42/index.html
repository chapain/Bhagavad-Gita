Bhagavad Gita 18.42 — open in the app →

शमो दमस्तपः शौचं क्षान्तिरार्जवमेव च । ज्ञानं विज्ञानमास्तिक्यं ब्रह्मकर्म स्वभावजम्

Tranquillity, self-control, austerity, purity, patience, uprightness, knowledge, wisdom and faith — the duty of the brāhmaṇa, born of his nature.
