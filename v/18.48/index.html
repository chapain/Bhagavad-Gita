सहजं कर्म कौन्तेय सदोषमपि न त्यजेत् । सर्वारम्भा हि दोषेण धूमेनाग्निरिवावृताः

One should not abandon one's natural work, O son of Kuntī, though it be faulty; for all undertakings are covered with fault, as fire with smoke.
