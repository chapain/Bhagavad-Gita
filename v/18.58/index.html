Bhagavad Gita 18.58 — open in the app →

मच्चित्तः सर्वदुर्गाणि मत्प्रसादात्तरिष्यसि । अथ चेत्त्वमहंकारान्न श्रोष्यसि विनङ्क्ष्यसि

With your mind on me, you shall cross all difficulties by my grace; but if from ego you will not listen, you shall be destroyed.
