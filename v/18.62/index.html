Bhagavad Gita 18.62 — open in the app →

तमेव शरणं गच्छ सर्वभावेन भारत । तत्प्रसादात्परां शान्तिं स्थानं प्राप्स्यसि शाश्वतम्

Take refuge in him alone with all your being, O Bhārata; by his grace you shall attain supreme peace and the eternal abode.
