Bhagavad Gita 18.68 — open in the app →

य इमं परमं गुह्यं मद्भक्तेष्वभिधास्यति । भक्तिं मयि परां कृत्वा मामेवैष्यत्यसंशयः

He who teaches this supreme secret to my devotees with supreme devotion — having given me his highest love, he shall come to me.
