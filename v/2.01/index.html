सञ्जय उवाच । तं तथा कृपयाविष्टमश्रुपूर्णाकुलेक्षणम् । विषीदन्तमिदं वाक्यमुवाच मधुसूदनः

Sañjaya said: Seeing him thus overcome with pity, his eyes filled with tears and agitation, the sorrowing Arjuna — Madhusūdana spoke these words.
