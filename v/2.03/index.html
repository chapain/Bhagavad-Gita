Bhagavad Gita 2.03 — open in the app →

क्लैब्यं मा स्म गमः पार्थ नैतत्त्वय्युपपद्यते । क्षुद्रं हृदयदौर्बल्यं त्यक्त्वोत्तिष्ठ परन्तप

Yield not to unmanliness, O Pārtha — it does not become you. Cast off this petty faintness of heart and arise, O scorcher of foes!
