Bhagavad Gita 2.13 — open in the app →

देहिनोऽस्मिन्यथा देहे कौमारं यौवनं जरा । तथा देहान्तरप्राप्तिर्धीरस्तत्र न मुह्यति

As the embodied soul passes in this body through childhood, youth and old age, so it passes into another body; the wise are not deluded by this.
