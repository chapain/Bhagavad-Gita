मात्रास्पर्शास्तु कौन्तेय शीतोष्णसुखदुःखदाः । आगमापायिनोऽनित्यास्तांस्तितिक्षस्व भारत

The contacts of the senses with their objects, O son of Kuntī, give cold and heat, pleasure and pain; they come and go, impermanent — endure them, O Bhārata.
