Bhagavad Gita 2.19 — open in the app →

य एनं वेत्ति हन्तारं यश्चैनं मन्यते हतम् । उभौ तौ न विजानीतो नायं हन्ति न हन्यते

Whoever thinks the Self is the killer, and whoever thinks it is killed — neither truly knows: it does not kill, nor is it killed.
