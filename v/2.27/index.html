Bhagavad Gita 2.27 — open in the app →

जातस्य हि ध्रुवो मृत्युर्ध्रुवं जन्म मृतस्य च । तस्मादपरिहार्येऽर्थे न त्वं शोचितुमर्हसि

For death is certain for the born, and birth is certain for the dead. Therefore, in an unavoidable matter, you should not grieve.
