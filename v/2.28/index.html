Bhagavad Gita 2.28 — open in the app →

अव्यक्तादीनि भूतानि व्यक्तमध्यानि भारत । अव्यक्तनिधनान्येव तत्र का परिदेवना

All beings are unmanifest in their beginning, manifest in their middle, and unmanifest again in their end. What is there to lament, O Bhārata?
