Bhagavad Gita 2.41 — open in the app →

व्यवसायात्मिका बुद्धिरेकेह कुरुनन्दन । बहुशाखा ह्यनन्ताश्च बुद्धयोऽव्यवसायिनाम्

The intellect of the resolute is single and one-pointed here, O joy of the Kurus; but the intellects of the irresolute branch out endlessly.
