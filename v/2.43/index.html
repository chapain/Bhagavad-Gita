कामात्मानः स्वर्गपरा जन्मकर्मफलप्रदाम् । क्रियाविशेषबहुलां भोगैश्वर्यगतिं प्रति

Desire-filled, heaven-seeking, they promise birth and karma as their fruit, and preach many special rites for the sake of pleasure and power.
