Bhagavad Gita 2.44 — open in the app →

भोगैश्वर्यप्रसक्तानां तयापहृतचेतसाम् । व्यवसायात्मिका बुद्धिः समाधौ न विधीयते

For those attached to pleasure and power, whose minds are carried away by such words, the one-pointed intellect of samādhi does not arise.
