Bhagavad Gita 2.50 — open in the app →

बुद्धियुक्तो जहातीह उभे सुकृतदुष्कृते । तस्माद्योगाय युज्यस्व योगः कर्मसु कौशलम्

One joined to the intellect leaves behind here both good deeds and bad. Therefore devote yourself to yoga — yoga is skill in action.
