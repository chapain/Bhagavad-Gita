Bhagavad Gita 2.51 — open in the app →

कर्मजं बुद्धियुक्ता हि फलं त्यक्त्वा मनीषिणः । जन्मबन्धविनिर्मुक्ताः पदं गच्छन्त्यनामयम्

The wise, united with the intellect and renouncing the fruits of their actions, are freed from the bonds of birth and reach the place beyond all ill.
