Bhagavad Gita 2.52 — open in the app →

यदा ते मोहकलिलं बुद्धिर्व्यतितरिष्यति । तदा गन्तासि निर्वेदं श्रोतव्यस्य श्रुतस्य च

When your intellect crosses beyond the mire of delusion, you will grow indifferent to what has been heard and what is yet to be heard.
