Bhagavad Gita 2.56 — open in the app →

दुःखेष्वनुद्विग्नमनाः सुखेषु विगतस्पृहः । वीतरागभयक्रोधः स्थितधीर्मुनिरुच्यते

One whose mind is untroubled in sorrow and free from longing in joy, from whom passion, fear and anger have departed — that one is the steady-minded sage.
