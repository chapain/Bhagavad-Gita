Bhagavad Gita 2.57 — open in the app →

यः सर्वत्रानभिस्नेहस्तत्तत्प्राप्य शुभाशुभम् । नाभिनन्दति न द्वेष्टि तस्य प्रज्ञा प्रतिष्ठिता

One who is without affection everywhere, who neither rejoices nor recoils on meeting good or evil — that one's wisdom is firmly set.
