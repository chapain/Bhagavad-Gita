Bhagavad Gita 2.58 — open in the app →

यदा संहरते चायं कूर्मोऽङ्गानीव सर्वशः । इन्द्रियाणीन्द्रियार्थेभ्यस्तस्य प्रज्ञा प्रतिष्ठिता

When, like a tortoise drawing in its limbs from all sides, one withdraws the senses from their objects — that one's wisdom is firmly set.
