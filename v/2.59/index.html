Bhagavad Gita 2.59 — open in the app →

विषया विनिवर्तन्ते निराहारस्य देहिनः । रसवर्जं रसोऽप्यस्य परं दृष्ट्वा निवर्तते

The objects fall away from the self-controlled one, but the taste for them remains; and when the Supreme is seen, even that taste departs.
