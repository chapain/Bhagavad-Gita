क्रोधाद्भवति सम्मोहः सम्मोहात्स्मृतिविभ्रमः । स्मृतिभ्रंशाद् बुद्धिनाशो बुद्धिनाशात्प्रणश्यति

From anger comes delusion; from delusion, confusion of memory; from broken memory, the ruin of understanding; and from that ruin, one is destroyed.
