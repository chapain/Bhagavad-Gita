रागद्वेषवियुक्तैस्तु विषयानिन्द्रियैश्चरन् । आत्मवश्यैर्विधेयात्मा प्रसादमधिगच्छति

But one who moves among objects with the senses mastered, free from attraction and aversion, self-controlled — that one attains serenity.
