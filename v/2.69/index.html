Bhagavad Gita 2.69 — open in the app →

या निशा सर्वभूतानां तस्यां जागर्ति संयमी । यस्यां जाग्रति भूतानि सा निशा पश्यतो मुनेः

What is night for all beings is the waking time for the disciplined sage; what is waking for beings is night for the sage who sees.
