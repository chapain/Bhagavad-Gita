Bhagavad Gita 2.70 — open in the app →

आपूर्यमाणमचलप्रतिष्ठं समुद्रमापः प्रविशन्ति यद्वत् । तद्वत्कामा यं प्रविशन्ति सर्वे स शान्तिमाप्नोति न कामकामी

As the waters enter the ocean, which remains unmoved though filled from every side, so the sage whom all desires enter remains at peace — not the one who lusts for desires.
