Bhagavad Gita 3.2 — open in the app →

व्यामिश्रेणेव वाक्येन बुद्धिं मोहयसीव मे । तदेकं वद निश्चित्य येन श्रेयोऽहमाप्नुयाम्

With words that appear equivocal you seem to confuse my intellect. Tell me one thing decisively, by which I may attain the good.
