श्रीभगवानुवाच । लोकेऽस्मिन्द्विविधा निष्ठा पुरा प्रोक्ता मयानघ । ज्ञानयोगेन साङ्ख्यानां कर्मयोगेन योगिनाम्

The Blessed Lord said: In this world a twofold path of steadfastness was declared by me of old, O sinless one: the yoga of knowledge for the Sāṅkhyas, and the yoga of action for the yogis.
