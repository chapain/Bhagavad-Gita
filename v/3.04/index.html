Bhagavad Gita 3.4 — open in the app →

न कर्मणामनारम्भान्नैष्कर्म्यं पुरुषोऽश्नुते । न च सन्न्यसनादेव सिद्धिं समधिगच्छति

Not by the non-performance of actions does one attain freedom from action, nor by mere renunciation alone does one reach perfection.
