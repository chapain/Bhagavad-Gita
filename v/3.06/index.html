Bhagavad Gita 3.6 — open in the app →

कर्मेन्द्रियाणि संयम्य य आस्ते मनसा स्मरन् । इन्द्रियार्थान्विमूढात्मा मिथ्याचारः स उच्यते

He who, restraining the organs of action, sits recollecting sense objects in his mind — that deluded one is called a hypocrite.
