Bhagavad Gita 3.08 — open in the app →

नियतं कुरु कर्म त्वं कर्म ज्यायो ह्यकर्मणः । शरीरयात्रापि च ते न प्रसिध्येदकर्मणः

Perform your prescribed action, for action is superior to inaction; even the maintenance of the body would not be accomplished by inaction.
