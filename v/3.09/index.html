यज्ञार्थात्कर्मणोऽन्यत्र लोकोऽयं कर्मबन्धनः । तदर्थं कर्म कौन्तेय मुक्तसङ्गः समाचर

The world is bound by action, except action performed for the sake of sacrifice; for that purpose, O son of Kuntī, perform action free from attachment.
