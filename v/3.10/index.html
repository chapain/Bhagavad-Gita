सहयज्ञाः प्रजाः सृष्ट्वा पुरोवाच प्रजापतिः । अनेन प्रसविष्यध्वमेष वोऽस्त्विष्टकामधुक्

Having created creatures together with sacrifice from the beginning, Prajāpati declared: “By this shall you prosper; may this be to you the cow of desires.”
