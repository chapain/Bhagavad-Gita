Bhagavad Gita 3.12 — open in the app →

इष्टान्भोगान्हि वो देवा दास्यन्ते यज्ञभाविताः । तैर्दत्तानप्रदायैभ्यो यो भुङ्क्ते स्तेन एव सः

Nourished by sacrifice, the gods will give you the joys you desire; he who enjoys what they have given without offering back to them is verily a thief.
