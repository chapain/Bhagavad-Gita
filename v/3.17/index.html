Bhagavad Gita 3.17 — open in the app →

यस्त्वात्मरतिरेव स्यादात्मतृप्तश्च मानवः । आत्मन्येव च सन्तुष्टस्तस्य कार्यं न विद्यते

But the man who rejoices only in the Self, is gratified with the Self, and is content in the Self alone — for him there is nothing to do.
