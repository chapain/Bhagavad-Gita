Bhagavad Gita 3.19 — open in the app →

तस्मादसक्तः सततं कार्यं कर्म समाचर । असक्तो ह्याचरन्कर्म परमाप्नोति पूरुषः

Therefore, always perform the action that is your duty, unattached; for performing action without attachment, a man attains the Supreme.
