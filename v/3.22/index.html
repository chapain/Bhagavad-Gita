Bhagavad Gita 3.22 — open in the app →

न मे पार्थास्ति कर्तव्यं त्रिषु लोकेषु किञ्चन । नानवाप्तमवाप्तव्यं वर्त एव च कर्मणि

For me, O Pārtha, there is nothing to be done in the three worlds, nothing unattained to be attained — yet I continue to act.
