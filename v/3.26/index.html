Bhagavad Gita 3.26 — open in the app →

न बुद्धिभेदं जनयेदज्ञानां कर्मसङ्गिनाम् । जोषयेत्सर्वकर्माणि विद्वान्युक्तः समाचरन्

Let him not create disturbance in the minds of the ignorant who are attached to action; acting himself with devotion, let him make them do all actions.
