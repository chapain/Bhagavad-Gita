तत्त्ववित्तु महाबाहो गुणकर्मविभागयोः । गुणा गुणेषु वर्तन्ते इति मत्वा न सज्जते

But the knower of truth, O mighty-armed, knowing the distinction of the guṇas and actions, and that the guṇas move among the guṇas, does not become attached.
