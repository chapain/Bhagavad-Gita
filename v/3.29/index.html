Bhagavad Gita 3.29 — open in the app →

प्रकृतेर्गुणसम्मूढाः सज्जन्ते गुणकर्मसु । तानकृत्स्नविदो मन्दान्कृत्स्नविन्न विचालयेत्

Those deluded by the guṇas of prakṛti cling to actions born of the guṇas; the one of complete knowledge should not unsettle the dull-witted, partial knowers.
