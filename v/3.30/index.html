Bhagavad Gita 3.30 — open in the app →

मयि सर्वाणि कर्माणि सन्न्यस्याध्यात्मचेतसा । निराशीर्निर्ममो भूत्वा युध्यस्व विगतज्वरः

Renouncing all actions in me, with the mind fixed on the Self, being free from desire and from possessiveness, fight — free from fever.
