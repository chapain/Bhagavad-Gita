Bhagavad Gita 3.31 — open in the app →

ये मे मतमिदं नित्यमनुतिष्ठन्ति मानवाः । श्रद्धावन्तोऽनसूयन्तो मुच्यन्ते तेऽपि कर्मभिः

Those men who always follow this teaching of mine with faith, without finding fault, are released even from the bondage of action.
