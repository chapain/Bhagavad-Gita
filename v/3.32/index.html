Bhagavad Gita 3.32 — open in the app →

ये त्वेतदभ्यसूयन्तो नानुतिष्ठन्ति मे मतम् । सर्वज्ञानविमूढांस्तान्विद्धि नष्टानचेतसः

But those who, finding fault, do not follow this teaching of mine — know them as deprived of all wisdom, ruined, and without understanding.
