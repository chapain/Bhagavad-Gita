श्रेयान्स्वधर्मो विगुणः परधर्मात्स्वनुष्ठितात् । स्वधर्मे निधनं श्रेयः परधर्मो भयावहः

Better is one's own dharma, though defective, than another's well-performed; death in one's own dharma is better — another's dharma brings fear.
