इन्द्रियाणि पराण्याहुरिन्द्रियेभ्यः परं मनः । मनसस्तु परा बुद्धिर्यो बुद्धेः परतस्तु सः

They say the senses are higher; higher than the senses is the mind; higher than the mind is the intellect; but higher than the intellect is He (the Self).
