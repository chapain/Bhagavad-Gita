Bhagavad Gita 3.43 — open in the app →

एवं बुद्धेः परं बुद्ध्वा संस्तभ्यात्मानमात्मना । जहि शत्रुं महाबाहो कामरूपं दुरासदम्

Thus knowing Him who is beyond the intellect, restraining the self by the Self, O mighty-armed one, slay the enemy having the form of desire, so difficult to conquer.
