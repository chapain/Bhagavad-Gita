Bhagavad Gita 4.03 — open in the app →

स एवायं मया तेऽद्य योगः प्रोक्तः पुरातनः । भक्तोऽसि मे सखा चेति रहस्यं ह्येतदुत्तमम्

This same ancient yoga is now declared to you by me, because you are my devotee and friend — truly, this is the supreme secret.
