Bhagavad Gita 4.6 — open in the app →

अजोऽपि सन्नव्ययात्मा भूतानामीश्वरोऽपि सन् । प्रकृतिं स्वामधिष्ठाय सम्भवाम्यात्ममायया

Though unborn, though the imperishable Self, though the Lord of beings — resorting to my own prakṛti, I come into being through my own māyā.
