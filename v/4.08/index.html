Bhagavad Gita 4.08 — open in the app →

परित्राणाय साधूनां विनाशाय च दुष्कृताम् । धर्मसंस्थापनार्थाय सम्भवामि युगे युगे

For the protection of the good, for the destruction of the evil-doers, for the establishment of dharma, I take birth age after age.
