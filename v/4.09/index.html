Bhagavad Gita 4.9 — open in the app →

जन्म कर्म च मे दिव्यमेवं यो वेत्ति तत्त्वतः । त्यक्त्वा देहं पुनर्जन्म नैति मामेति सोऽर्जुन

He who thus knows in truth my divine birth and action — having abandoned the body, he is not born again; he comes to me, O Arjuna.
