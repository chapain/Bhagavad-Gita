वीतरागभयक्रोधा मन्मया मामुपाश्रिताः । बहवो ज्ञानतपसा पूता मद्भावमागताः

Free from attachment, fear and anger, filled with me, taking refuge in me, many — purified by the austerity of knowledge — have attained my state.
