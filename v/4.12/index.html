Bhagavad Gita 4.12 — open in the app →

काङ्क्षन्तः कर्मणां सिद्धिं यजन्त इह देवताः । क्षिप्रं हि मानुषे लोके सिद्धिर्भवति कर्मजा

Those who desire the success of actions worship the gods here; indeed, in this human world success born of action comes quickly.
