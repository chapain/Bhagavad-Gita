Bhagavad Gita 4.13 — open in the app →

चातुर्वर्ण्यं मया सृष्टं गुणकर्मविभागशः । तस्य कर्तारमपि मां विद्ध्यकर्तारमव्ययम्

The fourfold order was created by me according to the distinction of guṇas and actions; though I am its author, know me as the non-doer and imperishable.
