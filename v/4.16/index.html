Bhagavad Gita 4.16 — open in the app →

किं कर्म किमकर्मेति कवयोऽप्यत्र मोहिताः । तत्ते कर्म प्रवक्ष्यामि यज्ज्ञात्वा मोक्ष्यसेऽशुभात्

What is action, what is inaction — even the wise are bewildered here; I shall declare to you that action, knowing which you will be released from evil.
