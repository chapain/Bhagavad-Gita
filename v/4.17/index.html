कर्मणो ह्यपि बोद्धव्यं बोद्धव्यं च विकर्मणः । अकर्मणश्च बोद्धव्यं गहना कर्मणो गतिः

Indeed the nature of action must be understood, and of forbidden action too, and of inaction; mysterious is the course of action.
