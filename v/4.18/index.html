Bhagavad Gita 4.18 — open in the app →

कर्मण्यकर्म यः पश्येदकर्मणि च कर्म यः । स बुद्धिमान्मनुष्येषु स युक्तः कृत्स्नकर्मकृत्

He who sees inaction in action, and action in inaction, is intelligent among men; he is engaged in yoga and performs all actions.
