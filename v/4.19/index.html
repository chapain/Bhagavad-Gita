Bhagavad Gita 4.19 — open in the app →

यस्य सर्वे समारम्भाः कामसङ्कल्पवर्जिताः । ज्ञानाग्निदग्धकर्माणं तमाहुः पण्डितं बुधाः

Whose undertakings are all free from desire and selfish intention, and whose actions are burnt by the fire of knowledge — him the wise call a man of learning.
