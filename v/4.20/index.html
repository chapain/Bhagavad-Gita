Bhagavad Gita 4.20 — open in the app →

त्यक्त्वा कर्मफलासङ्गं नित्यतृप्तो निराश्रयः । कर्मण्यभिप्रवृत्तोऽपि नैव किञ्चित्करोति सः

Having abandoned attachment to the fruit of action, ever content, without any support — though engaged in action, he verily does nothing.
