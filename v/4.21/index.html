Bhagavad Gita 4.21 — open in the app →

निराशीर्यतचित्तात्मा त्यक्तसर्वपरिग्रहः । शारीरं केवलं कर्म कुर्वन्नाप्नोति किल्बिषम्

Free from desire, with the mind and self controlled, having given up all possessions, performing only bodily action, he does not incur sin.
