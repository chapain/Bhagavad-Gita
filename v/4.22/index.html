यदृच्छालाभसन्तुष्टो द्वन्द्वातीतो विमत्सरः । समः सिद्धावसिद्धौच कृत्वापि न निबध्यते

Content with what comes unsought, beyond the pairs of opposites, free from envy, equal in success and failure — even acting, he is not bound.
