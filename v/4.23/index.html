गतसङ्गस्य मुक्तस्य ज्ञानावस्थितचेतसः । यज्ञायाचरतः कर्म समग्रं प्रविलीयते

The actions of one free from attachment, liberated, whose mind is established in knowledge and who acts for sacrifice — they dissolve entirely.
