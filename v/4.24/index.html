ब्रह्मार्पणं ब्रह्म हविर्ब्रह्माग्नौ ब्रह्मणा हुतम् । ब्रह्मैव तेन गन्तव्यं ब्रह्मकर्मसमाधिना

The ladle is Brahman, the oblation is Brahman, offered in the fire of Brahman by Brahman; Brahman alone is reached by him who is absorbed in the action of Brahman.
