सर्वाणीन्द्रियकर्माणि प्राणकर्माणि चापरे । आत्मसंयमयोगाग्नौ जुह्वति ज्ञानदीपिते

And others offer all the activities of the senses and of the vital breaths into the fire of the yoga of self-restraint, kindled by knowledge.
