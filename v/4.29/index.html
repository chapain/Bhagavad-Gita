अपाने जुह्वति प्राणं प्राणेऽपानं तथापरे । प्राणापानगती रुद्ध्वा प्राणायामपरायणाः

Some offer the up-breath into the down-breath, and the down-breath into the up-breath, checking the courses of the breaths, devoted to breath-control.
