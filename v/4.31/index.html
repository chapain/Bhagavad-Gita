Bhagavad Gita 4.31 — open in the app →

यज्ञशिष्टामृतभुजो यान्ति ब्रह्म सनातनम् । नायं लोकोऽस्त्ययज्ञस्य कुतोऽन्यः कुरुसत्तम

Tasting the nectar of the remnant of sacrifice, they reach the eternal Brahman; this world is not for the non-sacrificer, how then the next, O best of the Kurus?
