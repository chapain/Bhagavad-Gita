Bhagavad Gita 4.32 — open in the app →

एवं बहुविधा यज्ञा वितता ब्रह्मणो मुखे । कर्मजान्विद्धि तान्सर्वानेवं ज्ञात्वा विमोक्ष्यसे

Thus manifold sacrifices are set forth in the scriptures; know them all as born of action — knowing this, you shall be released.
