Bhagavad Gita 4.35 — open in the app →

यज्ज्ञात्वा न पुनर्मोहमेवं यास्यसि पाण्डव । येन भूतान्यशेषेण द्रक्ष्यस्यात्मन्यथो मयि

Knowing that, O son of Pāṇḍu, you will not again fall under delusion; by it you will see all beings without exception in the Self, and also in me.
