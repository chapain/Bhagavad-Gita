श्रद्धावांल्लभते ज्ञानं तत्परः संयतेन्द्रियः । ज्ञानं लब्ध्वा परां शान्तिमचिरेणाधिगच्छति

The faithful man, intent on it, with senses subdued, attains knowledge; and having attained knowledge, he soon reaches supreme peace.
