अज्ञश्चाश्रद्दधानश्च संशयात्मा विनश्यति । नायं लोकोऽस्ति न परो न सुखं संशयात्मनः

But the ignorant man without faith, full of doubt, perishes; for the doubting soul there is neither this world, nor the next, nor happiness.
