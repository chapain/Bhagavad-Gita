Bhagavad Gita 4.41 — open in the app →

योगसन्न्यस्तकर्माणं ज्ञानसञ्छिन्नसंशयम् । आत्मवन्तं न कर्माणि निबध्नन्ति धनञ्जय

Actions do not bind him who has renounced actions through yoga, whose doubt is destroyed by knowledge, who is self-possessed, O Dhanañjaya.
