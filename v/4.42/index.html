Bhagavad Gita 4.42 — open in the app →

तस्मादज्ञानसम्भूतं हृत्स्थं ज्ञानासिनात्मनः । छित्त्वैनं संशयं योगमातिष्ठोत्तिष्ठ भारत

Therefore, cutting with the sword of knowledge this doubt born of ignorance that dwells in your heart, stand firm in yoga — arise, O Bhārata!
