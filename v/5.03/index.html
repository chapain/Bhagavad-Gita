Bhagavad Gita 5.3 — open in the app →

ज्ञेयः स नित्यसन्न्यासी यो न द्वेष्टि न काङ्क्षति । निर्द्वन्द्वो हि महाबाहो सुखं बन्धात्प्रमुच्यते

He is to be known as ever-renouncing who neither hates nor longs; free from the pairs of opposites, O mighty-armed one, he is easily released from bondage.
