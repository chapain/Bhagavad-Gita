Bhagavad Gita 5.7 — open in the app →

योगयुक्तो विशुद्धात्मा विजितात्मा जितेन्द्रियः । सर्वभूतात्मभूतात्मा कुर्वन्नपि न लिप्यते

Devoted to karma-yoga, of pure soul, self-conquered, senses conquered, the Self of all beings — though acting, he is not stained.
