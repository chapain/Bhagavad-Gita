Bhagavad Gita 5.10 — open in the app →

ब्रह्मण्याधाय कर्माणि सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा करोति यः । लिप्यते न स पापेन पद्मपत्रमिवाम्भसा

He who acts, offering all actions to Brahman and abandoning attachment, is not stained by sin, just as a lotus leaf is not wetted by water.
