Bhagavad Gita 5.11 — open in the app →

कायेन मनसा बुद्ध्या केवलैरिन्द्रियैरपि । योगिनः कर्म कुर्वन्ति सङ्गं त्यक्त्वात्मशुद्धये

The yogis perform action by body, mind, intellect, or even by the senses alone, having abandoned attachment, for the purification of the self.
