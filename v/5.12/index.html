Bhagavad Gita 5.12 — open in the app →

युक्तः कर्मफलं त्यक्त्वा शान्तिमाप्नोति नैष्ठिकीम् । अयुक्तः कामकारेण फले सक्तो निबध्यते

The one established in yoga, giving up the fruit of action, attains lasting peace; the one not established, impelled by desire, attached to the fruit, becomes bound.
