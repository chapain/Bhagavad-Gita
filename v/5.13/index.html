Bhagavad Gita 5.13 — open in the app →

सर्वकर्माणि मनसा सन्न्यस्यास्ते सुखं वशी । नवद्वारे पुरे देही नैव कुर्वन्न कारयन्

Having mentally renounced all actions, the self-controlled embodied one dwells happily in the city of nine gates, neither acting nor causing others to act.
