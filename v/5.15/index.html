Bhagavad Gita 5.15 — open in the app →

नादत्ते कस्यचित्पापं न चैव सुकृतं विभुः । अज्ञानेनावृतं ज्ञानं तेन मुह्यन्ति जन्तवः

The all-pervading One neither accepts the sin nor the merit of anyone; knowledge is enveloped by ignorance, and thereby beings are deluded.
