ज्ञानेन तु तदज्ञानं येषां नाशितमात्मनः । तेषामादित्यवज्ज्ञानं प्रकाशयति तत्परम्

But for those whose ignorance is destroyed by the knowledge of the Self, that knowledge shines forth like the sun, revealing the Supreme.
