तद्बुद्धयस्तदात्मानस्तन्निष्ठास्तत्परायणाः । गच्छन्त्यपुनरावृत्तिं ज्ञाननिर्धूतकल्मषाः

With their intellect in that, their self in that, their refuge in that, their goal that — they go without return, their sins cleansed by knowledge.
