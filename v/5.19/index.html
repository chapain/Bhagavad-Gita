Bhagavad Gita 5.19 — open in the app →

इहैव तैर्जितः सर्गो येषां साम्ये स्थितं मनः । निर्दोषं हि समं ब्रह्म तस्माद्ब्रह्मणि ते स्थिताः

Here itself the world of birth and death is conquered by those whose minds rest in equanimity; Brahman is flawless and equal; therefore they are established in Brahman.
