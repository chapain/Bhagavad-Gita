Bhagavad Gita 5.20 — open in the app →

न प्रहृष्येत्प्रियं प्राप्य नोद्विजेत्प्राप्य चाप्रियम् । स्थिरबुद्धिरसम्मूढो ब्रह्मविद्ब्रह्मणि स्थितः

He does not rejoice on obtaining what is dear, nor is he perturbed on obtaining what is not dear; with steady intellect, undeluded, the knower of Brahman abides in Brahman.
