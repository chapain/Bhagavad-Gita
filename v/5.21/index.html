Bhagavad Gita 5.21 — open in the app →

बाह्यस्पर्शेष्वसक्तात्मा विन्दत्यात्मनि यत्सुखम् । स ब्रह्मयोगयुक्तात्मा सुखमक्षयमश्नुते

With the self unattached to external contacts, he finds the happiness that is in the Self; joined with the yoga of Brahman, he enjoys bliss that is imperishable.
