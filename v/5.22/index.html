Bhagavad Gita 5.22 — open in the app →

ये हि संस्पर्शजा भोगा दुःखयोनय एव ते । आद्यन्तवन्तः कौन्तेय न तेषु रमते बुधः

Indeed, the pleasures born of contact with the senses are only wombs of sorrow; they have a beginning and an end, O son of Kuntī — the wise do not rejoice in them.
