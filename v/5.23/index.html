Bhagavad Gita 5.23 — open in the app →

शक्नोतीहैव यः सोढुं प्राक्छरीरविमोक्षणात् । कामक्रोधोद्भवं वेगं स युक्तः स सुखी नरः

He who, even here, before the release of the body, is able to endure the impulse born of desire and anger — he is yoked in yoga, he is a happy man.
