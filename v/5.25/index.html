Bhagavad Gita 5.25 — open in the app →

लभन्ते ब्रह्मनिर्वाणमृषयः क्षीणकल्मषाः । छिन्नद्वैधा यतात्मानः सर्वभूतहिते रताः

The seers whose sins are destroyed, whose doubts are cut, whose selves are controlled, who delight in the welfare of all beings — they attain Brahman-nirvāṇa.
