Bhagavad Gita 5.26 — open in the app →

कामक्रोधवियुक्तानां यतीनां यतचेतसाम् । अभितो ब्रह्मनिर्वाणं वर्तते विदितात्मनाम्

For the ascetics free from desire and anger, whose thoughts are controlled, who know the Self — Brahman-nirvāṇa is present all around.
