स्पर्शान्कृत्वा बहिर्बाह्यांश्चक्षुश्चैवान्तरे भ्रुवोः । प्राणापानौ समौ कृत्वा नासाभ्यन्तरचारिणौ

Turning the sense-objects outward, fixing the vision between the eyebrows, making the out-breath and in-breath flow evenly within the nostrils —
