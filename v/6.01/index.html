श्रीभगवानुवाच । अनाश्रितः कर्मफलं कार्यं कर्म करोति यः । स सन्न्यासी च योगी च न निरग्निर्न चाक्रियः

The Blessed Lord said: He who performs his bounden action without depending on its fruit — he is both a renouncer and a yogi, not he who is without fire and without rites.
