Bhagavad Gita 6.2 — open in the app →

यं सन्न्यासमिति प्राहुर्योगं तं विद्धि पाण्डव । न ह्यसन्न्यस्तसङ्कल्पो योगी भवति कश्चन

That which they call renunciation, know that to be yoga, O son of Pāṇḍu; for none becomes a yogi who has not renounced selfish intention.
