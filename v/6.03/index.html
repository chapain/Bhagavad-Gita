Bhagavad Gita 6.03 — open in the app →

आरुरुक्षोर्मुनेर्योगं कर्म कारणमुच्यते । योगारूढस्य तस्यैव शमः कारणमुच्यते

For the sage who wishes to ascend in yoga, action is said to be the means; for him who has ascended, quiescence is said to be the means.
