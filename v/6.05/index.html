Bhagavad Gita 6.05 — open in the app →

उद्धरेदात्मनात्मानं नात्मानमवसादयेत् । आत्मैव ह्यात्मनो बन्धुरात्मैव रिपुरात्मनः

Let one raise the self by the Self and not degrade the self; for the Self alone is the friend of the self, and the Self alone is the enemy of the self.
