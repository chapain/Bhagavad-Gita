Bhagavad Gita 6.6 — open in the app →

बन्धुरात्मात्मनस्तस्य येनात्मैवात्मना जितः । अनात्मनस्तु शत्रुत्वे वर्तेतात्मैव शत्रुवत्

For him who has conquered the self by the Self, the Self is a friend; but for him who has not conquered the self, the Self abides as an enemy, hostile.
