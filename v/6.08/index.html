ज्ञानविज्ञानतृप्तात्मा कूटस्थो विजितेन्द्रियः । युक्त इत्युच्यते योगी समलोष्टाश्मकाञ्चनः

He who is satisfied with knowledge and realization, unmoved, with senses conquered, to whom a lump of earth, a stone and gold are alike — he is called a yoked one.
