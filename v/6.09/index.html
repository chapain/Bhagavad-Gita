सुहृन्मित्रार्युदासीनमध्यस्थद्वेष्यबन्धुषु । साधुष्वपि च पापेषु समबुद्धिर्विशिष्यते

He who is the same toward well-wishers, friends, foes, the indifferent, neutrals, the hateful, kinsmen and the righteous — he excels.
