Bhagavad Gita 6.10 — open in the app →

योगी युञ्जीत सततमात्मानं रहसि स्थितः । एकाकी यतचित्तात्मा निराशीरपरिग्रहः

Let the yogi constantly discipline himself, abiding in a solitary place, alone, with mind and self controlled, free from desire and possessiveness.
