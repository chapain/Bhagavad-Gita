तत्रैकाग्रं मनः कृत्वा यतचित्तेन्द्रियक्रियः । उपविश्यासने युञ्ज्याद्योगमात्मविशुद्धये

making the mind one-pointed, restraining the activities of the mind and the senses, seated on that seat, let him practise yoga for the purification of the self.
