Bhagavad Gita 6.16 — open in the app →

नात्यश्नतस्तु योगोऽस्ति न चैकान्तमनश्नतः । न चातिस्वप्नशीलस्य जाग्रतो नैव चार्जुन

Verily yoga is not attained by him who eats too much nor by him who does not eat at all, nor by him who sleeps too much nor by him who keeps awake too much, O Arjuna.
