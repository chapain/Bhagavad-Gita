Bhagavad Gita 6.17 — open in the app →

युक्ताहारविहारस्य युक्तचेष्टस्य कर्मसु । युक्तस्वप्नावबोधस्य योगो भवति दुःखहा

For him who is temperate in food and recreation, temperate in his efforts, temperate in sleep and waking, yoga becomes the destroyer of sorrow.
