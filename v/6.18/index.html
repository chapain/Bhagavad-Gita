Bhagavad Gita 6.18 — open in the app →

यदा विनियतं चित्तमात्मन्येवावतिष्ठते । निःस्पृहः सर्वकामेभ्यो युक्त इत्युच्यते तदा

When the well-trained mind rests in the Self alone, free from longing for all objects of desire — then one is said to be yoked.
