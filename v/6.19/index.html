Bhagavad Gita 6.19 — open in the app →

यदा दीपो निवातस्थो नेङ्गते सोपमा स्मृता । योगिनो यतचित्तस्य युञ्जतो योगमात्मनः

As a lamp placed in a windless spot does not flicker — this simile is declared of a yogi of controlled mind, practising yoga of the Self.
