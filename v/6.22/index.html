Bhagavad Gita 6.22 — open in the app →

यं लब्ध्वा चापरं लाभं मन्यते नाधिकं ततः । यस्मिन्स्थितो न दुःखेन गुरुणापि विचाल्यते

— having gained which one deems no other gain superior, in which one is not disturbed even by great sorrow — that state is known as yoga, the sundering of union with sorrow.
