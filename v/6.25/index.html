Bhagavad Gita 6.25 — open in the app →

शनैः शनैरुपरमेद्बुद्ध्या धृतिगृहीतया । आत्मसंस्थं मनः कृत्वा न किञ्चिदपि चिन्तयेत्

— slowly, gradually, let him come to rest through the intellect held firm by resolve; and fixing the mind on the Self alone, let him think of nothing else.
