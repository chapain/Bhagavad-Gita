Bhagavad Gita 6.26 — open in the app →

यतो यतो निश्चरति मनश्चञ्चलमस्थिरम् । ततस्ततो नियम्यैतदात्मन्येव वशं नयेत्

From whatever cause the wavering, unsteady mind wanders away, from that alone let him withdraw it and bring it under the control of the Self.
