Bhagavad Gita 6.31 — open in the app →

सर्वभूतस्थितं यो मां भजत्येकत्वमास्थितः । सर्वथा वर्तमानोऽपि स योगी मयि वर्तते

Established in unity, the yogi who worships me abiding in all beings — abiding in that, he lives in me, whatever his way of life.
