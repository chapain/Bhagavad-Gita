Bhagavad Gita 6.32 — open in the app →

आत्मौपम्येन सर्वत्र समं पश्यति योऽर्जुन । सुखं वा यदि वा दुःखं स योगी परमो मतः

He who, through the likeness of the self, sees the same everywhere, in pleasure and in pain — he is deemed the supreme yogi, O Arjuna.
