श्रीभगवानुवाच । असंशयं महाबाहो मनो दुर्निग्रहं चलम् । अभ्यासेन तु कौन्तेय वैराग्येण च गृह्यते

The Blessed Lord said: Undoubtedly the mind is restless and hard to restrain, O mighty-armed one; but it is restrained, O son of Kuntī, by practice and by dispassion.
