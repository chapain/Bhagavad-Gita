Bhagavad Gita 6.36 — open in the app →

असंयतात्मना योगो दुष्प्राप इति मे मतिः । वश्यात्मना तु यतता शक्योऽवाप्तुमुपायतः

Yoga is hard to attain, I think, for one whose self is unrestrained; but for the self-controlled one who strives by the proper means, it is attainable.
