अर्जुन उवाच । अयतिः श्रद्धयोपेतो योगाच्चलितमानसः । अप्राप्य योगसंसिद्धिं कां गतिं कृष्ण गच्छति

Arjuna said: He who, full of faith but unable to control himself, whose mind has fallen away from yoga — not attaining perfection in yoga — what end does he reach, O Kṛṣṇa?
