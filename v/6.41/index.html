Bhagavad Gita 6.41 — open in the app →

प्राप्य पुण्यकृतां लोकानुषित्वा शाश्वतीः समाः । शुचीनां श्रीमतां गेहे योगभ्रष्टोऽभिजायते

Having attained the worlds of the righteous and dwelt there for immemorial years, he who fell from yoga is born again in the house of the pure and prosperous.
