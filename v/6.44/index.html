Bhagavad Gita 6.44 — open in the app →

पूर्वाभ्यासेन तेनैव ह्रियते ह्यवशोऽपि सः । जिज्ञासुरपि योगस्य शब्दब्रह्मातिवर्तते

By that very former practice he is carried along, even in spite of himself; even the one who merely wishes to know yoga transcends the mere word of the Vedas.
