तपस्विभ्योऽधिको योगी ज्ञानिभ्योऽपि मतोऽधिकः । कर्मिभ्यश्चाधिको योगी तस्माद्योगी भवार्जुन

The yogi is greater than the ascetic, deemed greater than the man of knowledge, and greater than the man of rites; therefore be a yogi, O Arjuna.
