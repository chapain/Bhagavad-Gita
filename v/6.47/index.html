योगिनामपि सर्वेषां मद्गतेनान्तरात्मना । श्रद्धावान्भजते यो मां स मे युक्ततमो मतः

And among all yogis, he who, full of faith, worships me with his inmost self established in me — him I deem the most yoked, in my view.
