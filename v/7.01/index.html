श्रीभगवानुवाच । मय्यासक्तमनाः पार्थ योगं युञ्जन्मदाश्रयः । असंशयं समग्रं मां यथा ज्ञास्यसि तच्छृणु

The Blessed Lord said: With the mind attached to me, O Pārtha, practising yoga and taking refuge in me — how, without doubt, you will know me in full, that listen.
