ज्ञानं तेऽहं सविज्ञानमिदं वक्ष्याम्यशेषतः । यज्ज्ञात्वा नेह भूयोऽन्यज्ज्ञातव्यमवशिष्यते

I shall declare to you this knowledge together with its realization, without remainder; knowing which, nothing further remains to be known here.
