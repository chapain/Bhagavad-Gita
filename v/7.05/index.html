Bhagavad Gita 7.5 — open in the app →

अपरेयमितस्त्वन्यां प्रकृतिं विद्धि मे पराम् । जीवभूतां महाबाहो ययेदं धार्यते जगत्

This is the lower nature; but know my other nature, higher than this, O mighty-armed one — the life-element, by which the world is upheld.
