बलं बलवतां चाहं कामरागविवर्जितम् । धर्माविरुद्धो भूतेषु कामोऽस्मि भरतर्षभ

And I am the strength of the strong, devoid of desire and attachment; and in beings I am desire that is not opposed to dharma, O bull of the Bhāratas.
