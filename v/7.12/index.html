Bhagavad Gita 7.12 — open in the app →

ये चैव सात्त्विका भावा राजसास्तमसाश्च ये । मत्त एवेति तान्विद्धि न त्वहं तेषु ते मयि

And whatever states there are, of sattva, rajas or tamas — know them all as from me alone; yet I am not in them, they are in me.
