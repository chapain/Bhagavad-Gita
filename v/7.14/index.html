Bhagavad Gita 7.14 — open in the app →

दैवी ह्येषा गुणमयी मम माया दुरत्यया । मामेव ये प्रपद्यन्ते मायामेतां तरन्ति ते

This divine māyā of mine, consisting of the guṇas, is hard to overcome; but those who take refuge in me alone cross beyond this māyā.
