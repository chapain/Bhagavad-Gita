Bhagavad Gita 7.15 — open in the app →

न मां दुष्कृतिनो मूढाः प्रपद्यन्ते नराधमाः । माययापहृतज्ञाना आसुरं भावमाश्रिताः

The wrong-doers, the deluded, the lowest of men do not resort to me; robbed of knowledge by māyā, they cling to a demoniac nature.
