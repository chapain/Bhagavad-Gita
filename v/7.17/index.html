Bhagavad Gita 7.17 — open in the app →

तेषां ज्ञानी नित्ययुक्त एकभक्तिर्विशिष्यते । प्रियो हि ज्ञानिनोऽत्यर्थमहं स च मम प्रियः

Of them, the man of knowledge, ever yoked, of single devotion, is the best; for I am extremely dear to the wise, and he is dear to me.
