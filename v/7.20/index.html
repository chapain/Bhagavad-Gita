कामैस्तैस्तैर्हृतज्ञानाः प्रपद्यन्तेऽन्यदेवताः । तं तं नियममास्थाय प्रकृत्या नियताः स्वया

Those whose knowledge is stolen by desires resort to other deities, following this or that rite, constrained by their own nature.
