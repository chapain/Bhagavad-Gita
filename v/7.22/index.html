Bhagavad Gita 7.22 — open in the app →

स तया श्रद्धया युक्तस्तस्या राधनमीहते । लभते च ततः कामान्मयैव विहितान्हि तान्

United with that faith, he engages in the worship of that deity, and from it obtains his desires — which are verily ordained by me.
