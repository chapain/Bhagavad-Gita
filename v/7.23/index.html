Bhagavad Gita 7.23 — open in the app →

अन्तवत्तु फलं तेषां तद्भवत्यल्पमेधसाम् । देवान्देवयजो यान्ति मद्भक्ता यान्ति मामपि

But the fruit of those worshippers is limited; the fruit belongs to the small-minded. The worshippers of the gods go to the gods; my devotees come to me.
