Bhagavad Gita 7.24 — open in the app →

अव्यक्तं व्यक्तिमापन्नं मन्यन्ते मामबुद्धयः । परं भावमजानन्तो ममाव्ययमनुत्तमम्

The unintelligent, not knowing my higher nature — imperishable and unsurpassed — think of me, the unmanifest, as one who has taken on manifestation.
