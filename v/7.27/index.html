Bhagavad Gita 7.27 — open in the app →

इच्छाद्वेषसमुत्थेन द्वन्द्वमोहेन भारत । सर्वभूतानि सम्मोहं सर्गे यान्ति परन्तप

By the delusion of the pairs of opposites, arising from desire and aversion, O Bhārata, all beings go to delusion at birth, O scorcher of foes.
