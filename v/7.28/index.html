येषां त्वन्तगतं पापं जनानां पुण्यकर्मणाम् । ते द्वन्द्वमोहनिर्मुक्ता भजन्ते मां दृढव्रताः

But those men of good deeds whose sins have come to an end — freed from the delusion of the pairs of opposites — worship me with firm vows.
