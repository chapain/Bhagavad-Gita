साधिभूताधिदैवं मां साधियज्ञं च ये विदुः । प्रयाणकालेऽपि च मां ते विदुर्युक्तचेतसः

Those who know me along with the elements, the deities and the sacrifice — even at the time of death, with minds established, they know me.
