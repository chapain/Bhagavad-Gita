श्रीभगवानुवाच । अक्षरं ब्रह्म परमं स्वभावोऽध्यात्ममुच्यते । भूतभावोद्भवकरो विसर्गः कर्मसंज्ञितः

The Blessed Lord said: The imperishable is the supreme Brahman; one's own nature is called adhyātma; the creative impulse that brings forth beings is called karma.
