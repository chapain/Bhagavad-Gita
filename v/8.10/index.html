Bhagavad Gita 8.10 — open in the app →

प्रयाणकाले मनसाचलेन भक्त्या युक्तो योगबलेन चैव । भ्रुवोर्मध्ये प्राणमावेश्य सम्य क्स तं परं पुरुषमुपैति दिव्यम्

— at the time of death, with an unwavering mind, united with devotion and the power of yoga, breath fixed between the brows — he reaches that supreme divine Person.
