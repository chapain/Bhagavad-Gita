Bhagavad Gita 8.11 — open in the app →

यदक्षरं वेदविदो वदन्ति विशन्ति यद्यतयो वीतरागाः । यदिच्छन्तो ब्रह्मचर्यं चरन्ति तत्ते पदं सङ्ग्रहेण प्रवक्ष्ये

That which the knowers of the Vedas call the imperishable, which the desireless ascetics enter, desiring which they practise celibacy — that goal I shall declare to you briefly.
