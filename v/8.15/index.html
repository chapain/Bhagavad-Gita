Bhagavad Gita 8.15 — open in the app →

मामुपेत्य पुनर्जन्म दुःखालयमशाश्वतम् । नाप्नुवन्ति महात्मानः संसिद्धिं परमां गताः

Having reached me, the great souls do not return to birth — the abode of sorrow, impermanent — they have attained the highest perfection.
