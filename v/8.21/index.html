Bhagavad Gita 8.21 — open in the app →

अव्यक्तोऽक्षर इत्युक्तस्तमाहुः परमां गतिम् । यं प्राप्य न निवर्तन्ते तद्धाम परमं मम

That unmanifest is called the imperishable; they call it the supreme goal; reaching which they do not return — that is my supreme abode.
