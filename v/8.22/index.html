Bhagavad Gita 8.22 — open in the app →

पुरुषः स परः पार्थ भक्त्या लभ्यस्त्वनन्यया । यस्यान्तःस्थानि भूतानि येन सर्वमिदं ततम्

That supreme Person, O Pārtha, is attainable by devotion to him alone; in him all beings dwell, and by him all this is pervaded.
