अग्निर्ज्योतिरहः शुक्लः षण्मासा उत्तरायणम् । तत्र प्रयाता गच्छन्ति ब्रह्म ब्रह्मविदो जनाः

Fire, light, day, the bright fortnight, the six months of the northern course — departing by that path, the knowers of Brahman go to Brahman.
