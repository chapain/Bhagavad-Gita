शुक्लकृष्णे गती ह्येते जगतः शाश्वते मते । एकया यात्यनावृत्तिमन्ययावर्तते पुनः

These two paths, bright and dark, are the eternal paths of the world; by one, one goes without return, by the other one returns again.
