श्रीभगवानुवाच । इदं तु ते गुह्यतमं प्रवक्ष्याम्यनसूयवे । ज्ञानं विज्ञानसहितं यज्ज्ञात्वा मोक्ष्यसेऽशुभात्

The Blessed Lord said: To you, who do not find fault, I will declare this profoundest secret — knowledge together with its realization; knowing which, you shall be freed from evil.
