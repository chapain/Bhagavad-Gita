Bhagavad Gita 9.17 — open in the app →

पिताहमस्य जगतो माता धाता पितामहः । वेद्यं पवित्रमोङ्कार ऋक्साम यजुरेव च

I am the father and mother of this world, its supporter and grandsire; I am the object of knowledge, the purifier, the syllable Om, and the Ṛk, Sāma and Yajus.
