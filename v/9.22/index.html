अनन्याश्चिन्तयन्तो मां ये जनाः पर्युपासते । तेषां नित्याभियुक्तानां योगक्षेमं वहाम्यहम्

To those who worship me, ever united, thinking of me alone — for them, ever devoted, I myself carry what they lack and preserve what they have.
