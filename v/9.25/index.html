Bhagavad Gita 9.25 — open in the app →

यान्ति देवव्रता देवान्पितॄन्यान्ति पितृव्रताः । भूतानि यान्ति भूतेज्या यान्ति मद्याजिनोऽपि माम्

The worshippers of the gods go to the gods; the worshippers of the ancestors go to the ancestors; the worshippers of the spirits go to the spirits; but my worshippers come to me.
