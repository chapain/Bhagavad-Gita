Bhagavad Gita 9.27 — open in the app →

यत्करोषि यदश्नासि यज्जुहोषि ददासि यत् । यत्तपस्यसि कौन्तेय तत्कुरुष्व मदर्पणम्

Whatever you do, whatever you eat, whatever you offer, whatever you give, whatever austerity you perform, O son of Kuntī — do it as an offering to me.
