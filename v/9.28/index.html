Bhagavad Gita 9.28 — open in the app →

शुभाशुभफलैरेवं मोक्ष्यसे कर्मबन्धनैः । सन्न्यासयोगयुक्तात्मा विमुक्तो मामुपैष्यसि

Thus you shall be freed from the bonds of actions, which bear good and evil fruit; with the self yoked in renunciation, liberated, you shall come to me.
