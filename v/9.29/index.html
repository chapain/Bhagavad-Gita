Bhagavad Gita 9.29 — open in the app →

समोऽहं सर्वभूतेषु न मे द्वेष्योऽस्ति न प्रियः । ये भजन्ति तु मां भक्त्या मयि ते तेषु चाप्यहम्

I am the same to all beings; none is dear or hated by me; but those who worship me with devotion — they are in me, and I am in them.
