Bhagavad Gita 9.30 — open in the app →

अपि चेत्सुदुराचारो भजते मामनन्यभाक् । साधुरेव स मन्तव्यः सम्यग्व्यवसितो हि सः

Even if a man of very evil conduct worships me with single-minded devotion, he is to be regarded as good, for he is rightly resolved.
