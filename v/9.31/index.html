Bhagavad Gita 9.31 — open in the app →

क्षिप्रं भवति धर्मात्मा शश्वच्छान्तिं निगच्छति । कौन्तेय प्रतिजानीहि न मे भक्तः प्रणश्यति

Quickly he becomes righteous of soul and attains lasting peace; know this for certain, O son of Kuntī: my devotee never perishes.
