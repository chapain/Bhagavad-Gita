Bhagavad Gita 9.34 — open in the app →

मन्मना भव मद्भक्तो मद्याजी मां नमस्कुरु । मामेवैष्यसि युक्त्वैवमात्मानं मत्परायणः

Fix your mind on me, be my devotee, offer to me, bow to me — thus yoking the self to me, intent on me, you shall surely come to me.
